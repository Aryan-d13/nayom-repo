#### **What is Dental Sleep Medicine?**

Dental sleep medicine focuses on treating obstructive sleep apnea and related breathing conditions through custom oral appliance therapy rather than relying solely on a CPAP machine. A CPAP is highly effective but many patients find the mask uncomfortable, claustrophobic, or simply impossible to sleep with consistently. For those patients oral appliance therapy offers a clinically supported and FDA approved alternative that is small, comfortable, completely silent, and easy to travel with.

‍

#### **Signs You May Have Sleep Apnea**

Because sleep apnea happens while you are unconscious, many people live with it for years without realizing it. Common signs include loud or frequent snoring, gasping or pausing in breathing during sleep that a partner notices, waking up with a dry mouth or sore throat, morning headaches, persistent daytime fatigue no matter how many hours you slept, and difficulty concentrating or staying focused during the day. If any of these sound familiar a sleep apnea evaluation is absolutely worth pursuing.

‍

#### **Who Is a Good Candidate for Oral Appliance Therapy?**

Oral appliance therapy is particularly well suited for patients with mild to moderate obstructive sleep apnea, patients who have been diagnosed with sleep apnea but cannot tolerate their CPAP device, patients who travel frequently and need a portable solution, and patients who snore significantly even without a formal sleep apnea diagnosis. Dr. Pandya works closely with physicians and sleep specialists to make sure every patient receives a thorough evaluation and the most appropriate treatment for their specific situation.
