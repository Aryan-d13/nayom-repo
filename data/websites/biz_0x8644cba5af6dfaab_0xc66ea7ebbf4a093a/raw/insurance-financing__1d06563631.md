Flexible financing

## More ways to make care fit your *budget.*

For eligible out-of-pocket dental costs, Forest Family offers third-party financing options through CareCredit and Cherry. Ask our team which options are available for your treatment and location.

Financing partner 01

### CareCredit

CareCredit is a health and wellness credit card that can be used for eligible dental expenses at participating providers. Applications, credit decisions and financing terms are managed by CareCredit.

[Apply with CareCredit](#)

Subject to credit approval. Ask Forest Family which CareCredit options may be available before treatment.

Financing partner 02

### Cherry

Cherry offers a third-party payment-plan option for eligible treatment. Availability, approval, payment schedules and terms are provided by Cherry and may vary.

Subject to eligibility and approval. Contact your preferred office before applying or choosing a payment option.
