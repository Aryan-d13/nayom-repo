Questions, answered

## What to know before you begin

Your dentist will give you advice specific to your smile. These are the straightforward answers patients ask us most often.

### How do I get started with Invisalign?

Book a free consultation. We will listen to your goals, scan your teeth digitally and explain whether Invisalign is a good fit — including your likely timeline and exact investment.

### How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Timing depends on the movements your smile needs. Focused cosmetic cases may take six months or less, while comprehensive treatment often takes 6–12 months. Your scan allows us to give you a more personal estimate.

### What does Diamond Plus provider mean?

It reflects a high volume of Invisalign cases treated by the practice. For patients, that means working with doctors who have planned a broad range of smiles and can anticipate more of the details along the way.

### Is Invisalign available at every Forest Family office?

Yes. Digital scanning and Invisalign consultations are available across our five Austin and Round Rock locations, so you can choose the office that works best for you.

### Can I finance my treatment?

Yes. We can review available CareCredit options and help you understand any applicable insurance benefits before treatment begins.

### Will I need retainers after Invisalign?

Retainers are an important part of protecting your result. Your dentist will explain when and how to wear them based on your treatment plan.
