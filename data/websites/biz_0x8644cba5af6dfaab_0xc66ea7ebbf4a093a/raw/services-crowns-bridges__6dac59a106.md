#### **Dental Crowns and Bridges in Austin and Round Rock TX**

At Forest Family Dentistry we create custom dental crowns and bridges for patients across all five of our Austin and Round Rock locations. Whether you are dealing with a tooth that has been cracked, severly decayed, or weakened after a root canal, or you are missing one or more teeth and want a fixed solution that looks and feels completely natural, our team has the experience and technology to restore your smile beautifully.

‍

#### **What Is a Dental Crown?**

A dental crown is a custom made cap that fits over a damaged or weakened tooth, restoring its shape, strength, and appearance. Think of it as a protective cover that brings the tooth back to full function while looking completely natural alongside your other teeth.

Crowns are most commonly used to protect a tooth that has been cracked or significantly weakened, restore a tooth after a root canal, cover a tooth with a very large filling that is at risk of fracturing, anchor one end of a dental bridge, or improve the appearance of a tooth that is misshapen or severly discolored. We use durable tooth colored materials like porcelain and ceramic so your crown blends seamlessly with the rest of your smile.

‍

#### **What Is a Dental Bridge?**

A dental bridge is a fixed restoration that replaces one or more missing teeth. It consists of an artificial tooth or teeth held in place by crowns that are bonded to the natural teeth on either side of the gap, or in some cases supported by dental implants. Once placed a bridge is permanent and functions just like natural teeth for chewing and speaking.

Beyond restoring your smile aesthetically, a bridge plays an important functional role. When a tooth goes missing the surrounding teeth tend to shift over time to fill the space, which can affect your bite, your jaw, and even the shape of your face. A bridge keeps everything where it belongs.

‍

#### **Which Option Is Right For You?**

If your tooth is still in place but damaged or weakened a crown is usually the right answer. If a tooth is missing entirely and you want a fixed non-removable solution, a bridge is worth discussing. Dental implants are another excellent option for replacing missing teeth and our team can walk you through the differences at your consultation so you can make the decision that feels right for your situation and your budget.

‍

#### **What to Expect**

Getting a crown or bridge at Forest Family Dentistry typically takes two appointments. At the first visit we prepare the tooth, take impressions or digital scans, and place a temporary restoration. At the second visit we bond your permanent, custom made restoration in place and make any final adjustments. Our team works closely with high quality dental laboratories to make sure every restoration we deliver looks natural, fits precisely, and is built to last.
