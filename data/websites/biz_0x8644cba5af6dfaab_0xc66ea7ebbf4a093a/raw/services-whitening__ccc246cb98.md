#### **Zoom® Teeth Whitening in Austin and Round Rock TX**

At Forest Family Dentistry we offer Zoom**®** professional teeth whitening across all five of our Austin and Round Rock locations for patients who want a noticeably brighter smile quickly, safely, and without the guesswork of over the counter products. Zoom® is one of the most trusted and widely used professional whitening systems in the country and the results speak for themselves. Most patients leave their appointment with teeth that are up to eight shades whiter than when they arrived, and the difference is genuinely dramatic.

‍

#### **What Is Zoom® Whitening?**

Zoom® whitening is an in-office treatment that uses a professional strength hydrogen peroxide gel activated by a specially designed LED light to break up deep set stains inside the tooth structure. Because the gel is professionally formulated and the light accelerates the whitening process, Zoom reaches stains that over the counter strips and trays simply cannot touch, including the kind of deep discoloration that builds up over years of coffee, tea, wine, and natural aging.

‍

#### What to Expect at Your **Appointment**

The whole process takes about an hour and a half from start to finish and is completely comfortable for most patients. We begin by protecting your gums and lips so only your teeth are exposed to the whitening gel. We then apply the Zoom**®** gel to your enamel and activate it with the LED light. That cycle is repeated three to four times in sessions of about fifteen minutes each, and by the end of your last session your smile will already look dramatically different. Many patients are genuinely surprised by how fast it works.

‍

#### Your Take Home Kit Is Included

Every Zoom**®** whitening treatment at Forest Family Dentistry comes with a complete Philips Zoom® take home maintenance kit at no extra charge. You receive custom fitted whitening trays made specifically for your teeth, along with DayWhite and NiteWhite whitening gels and a storage case with easy to follow instructions. The take home kid lets you touch up your results on your own schedule and extend the life of your whitening significantly. It is one of the things that sets professional whitening apart from anything you can buy at the drugstore.

‍

#### How Long Do Results Last?

With normal care most patients enjoy their Zoom**®** results for one to three years. Avoiding heavy staining drinks like coffee, tea, and red wine right after treatment gives your enamel time to fully stabilize and helps the results last as long as possible. Using your take home kit for occasional touch-ups between visits keeps your smile looking consistently bright over time.

‍

#### Is Zoom**® Right for You?**

Zoom**®** Whiteing is a great fit for most adults who want faster and more dramatic results than at-home whitening can deliver. It is especially popular for patients getting ready for weddings, reunions, job interviews, or any occasion where they want to feel their most confident. If you have dental restorations like crowns or veneers it is worth nothing that whitening gel does not change the color of those materials, so we will discuss realistic expectations at your consultation to make sure you are happy with the outcome.

‍
