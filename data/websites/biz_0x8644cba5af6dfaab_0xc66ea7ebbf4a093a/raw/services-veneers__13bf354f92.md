#### **Dental Veneers in Austin and Round Rock TX**

‍

#### **What Are Dental Veneers?**

Dental veneers are ultra-thin custom shells made from porcelain or high quality composite material that are bonded to the front surfaces of the teeth to improve their shape, size, color, and overall appearance. They are strong, stain resistant, and designed to blend seamlessly with your surrounding teeth so that no one can tell where your natural tooth ends and the veneer begins.

Veneers are an excellent solution for teeth that are chipped or cracked, severly stained or discolored in ways that whitening cannot address, worn down from grinding or aging, slightly misaligned or uneven in shape, or have small gaps between them that affect the overall look of the smile.

‍

#### **Traditional Porcelain Veneers**

Porcelain veneers are the gold standard of cosmetic smile transformation. Each veneer is custom crafted to match the exact shape, size, and shape that suits your face and your goals. The process typically takes two appointments. At the first visit we prepare the tooth by removing a small amount of enamel, take impressiona or digital scans, and place a temporary veneer. At the second visit your permanent, laboratory crafted veneers are bonded in place and any final adjustments are made. The results are beautiful, durable, and built to last well over a decade with proper care.

‍

#### **HALO® Veneers: No-Prep, No-Injection, No Downtime**

For patients who want the look of veneers without any enamel removal or anesthesia, HALO® Veneers offer a remarkable conservative alternative. **Available at our Anderson Lane and Round Rock locations**, HALO® Veneers use carefully layered and sculpted composite material to create a symmetrical, radiant smile without any drilling or injections at all. The entire transformation can often be completed in a single visit and because no tooth structure is removed the treatment is completely reversible if you ever want to change course. They are ideal for patients who want meaningful cosmetic improvement with the least invasive approach possible.

‍

#### Why Option Is Right for You?

The best veneers option depends on what you are hoping to correct, how much change you are looking for, and your personal preferences around the treatment process. Some patients are great candidates for HALO**®** Veneers and love the idea of walking out the same day with a transformed smile. Others have more significant concerns that call for the strength and customization of traditional porcelain. At your consultation our team will listen to your goals, evaluate your teeth honestly, and help you decide which approach makes the most sense for your situation without pushing you toward anything you are not comfortable with.
