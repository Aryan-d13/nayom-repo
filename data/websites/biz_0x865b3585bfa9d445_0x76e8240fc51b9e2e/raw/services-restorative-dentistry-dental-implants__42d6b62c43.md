## What Are Dental Implants?

A dental implant is a titanium post placed into the jawbone to serve as a new root for a missing tooth. Once integrated, it supports a custom crown, bridge, or denture—creating a replacement that looks and functions like a natural tooth.

Dental implants may be recommended for:

* ◆ Single or multiple missing teeth
* ◆ Support for [**Dental Bridges**](/services/restorative-dentistry/dental-bridges) or full-arch restorations
* ◆ Replacing a failing tooth that cannot be saved
* ◆ Providing stability for [**Implant-Supported Dentures**](/services/restorative-dentistry/implant-supported-dentures)
* ◆ Patients seeking a long-term, fixed alternative to removable solutions

The result is a restoration that doesn’t shift, doesn’t limit your lifestyle, and doesn’t feel like a replacement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long do dental implants last?


With proper care, dental implants can last decades—often a lifetime. Routine hygiene and regular checkups help ensure longevity.

### Do dental implants look natural?


Yes. Once restored with a custom crown or bridge, your implant is designed to blend seamlessly with your smile.

### Is the procedure painful?


The implant procedure is performed with comfort in mind. Most patients experience minimal discomfort and are surprised by how easy recovery can be.

### Am I a candidate for implants?


If you’re in good general and oral health with adequate bone support, you’re likely a candidate. A consultation will confirm if implants are right for you.
