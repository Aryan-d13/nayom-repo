## When a Crown Is the Right Choice

A crown is a full-coverage restoration that surrounds the entire visible portion of the tooth, offering both protection and cosmetic improvement. It's often used when a tooth has been structurally compromised, or when prior treatments no longer provide support.

A dental crown may be recommended for:

* ◆ Cracked or fractured teeth
* ◆ Teeth with large fillings or recurrent decay
* ◆ Restoration after root canal therapy
* ◆ Teeth with significant wear or erosion
* ◆ Cosmetic enhancement of misshapen or discolored teeth

Each crown is custom-made to match the surrounding teeth in color, shape, and contour—resulting in a restoration that feels invisible in daily life.

## Materials and Options

We offer both **porcelain** and **ceramic crowns**, selected based on your needs and the location of the tooth:

* ◆ **Porcelain crowns** offer high strength with a beautiful, lifelike appearance—ideal for visible front teeth where aesthetics are paramount.
* ◆ **All-ceramic crowns** combine durability with translucency and are often used when metal-free restorations are preferred.

In some cases, crowns may be part of a broader treatment plan, such as a [**Full Mouth Reconstruction**](/services/restorative-dentistry/full-mouth-reconstruction) or paired with [**Dental Implants**](/services/restorative-dentistry/dental-implants) to replace a missing tooth entirely.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long do dental crowns last?


With excellent care, crowns can last 10–15 years or more. Regular cleanings and avoiding habits like grinding or chewing on hard objects help extend their lifespan.

### Will the crown match my natural teeth?


Yes. We carefully select the material and shade to match your surrounding teeth, ensuring the crown looks and feels like part of your natural smile.

### Is the procedure painful?


The process is gentle and typically involves local anesthesia. Most patients experience minimal discomfort and return to normal activities the same day.

### Can crowns improve the look of my smile?


Absolutely. In addition to restoring strength, crowns can enhance symmetry, close gaps, and correct discoloration—often as part of a [**Smile Makeover**](/services/cosmetic-dentistry/smile-makeovers).
