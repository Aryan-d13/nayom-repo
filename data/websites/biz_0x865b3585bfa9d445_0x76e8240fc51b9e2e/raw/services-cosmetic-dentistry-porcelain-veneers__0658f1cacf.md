## What Porcelain Veneers Can Do

Porcelain veneers are thin, custom-crafted shells bonded to the front of your teeth. They are ideal for correcting:

* ◆ Discoloration that resists whitening
* ◆ Uneven spacing or gaps
* ◆ Chips, cracks, or worn enamel
* ◆ Irregular shapes or asymmetry
* ◆ Mild alignment issues

The final result is subtle yet striking. Veneers reflect light like natural enamel and are color-matched to your overall facial tone—not just your teeth. The goal isn’t to make you look different. It’s to make you look like you, only brighter.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long do porcelain veneers last?


With excellent care, porcelain veneers can last 10 to 15 years or more. Their lifespan depends on oral hygiene habits and avoiding habits like teeth grinding or using teeth as tools.

### Are porcelain veneers painful to get?


Most patients experience little to no discomfort. A small amount of enamel is removed, but the process is gentle and typically requires only local anesthesia.

### Do veneers look natural?


Yes. High-quality veneers are crafted to match the translucency and shape of natural teeth. We carefully customize every detail to suit your smile and facial features.

### What’s the difference between veneers and crowns?


Veneers cover only the front surface of the tooth, ideal for cosmetic enhancements. Crowns cover the entire tooth and are used when more structural support is needed.
