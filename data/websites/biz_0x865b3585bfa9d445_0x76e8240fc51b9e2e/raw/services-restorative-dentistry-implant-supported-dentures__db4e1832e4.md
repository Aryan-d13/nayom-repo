## What Are Implant-Supported Dentures?

Implant-supported dentures are custom prosthetics that attach to dental implants placed in the jawbone. Unlike removable dentures, they stay in place—creating a stable, comfortable, and natural-feeling solution for full-arch tooth loss.

You may be a candidate if:

* ◆ You’ve lost most or all teeth on the upper or lower arch
* ◆ You want a secure, non-removable alternative to traditional dentures
* ◆ You’ve experienced discomfort or looseness with removable prosthetics
* ◆ You want to preserve jawbone health and facial structure
* ◆ You’re looking for a long-term solution with improved bite function

By integrating directly with the jaw, implant-supported dentures restore more than just the appearance of your smile—they provide the strength to eat, speak, and laugh without hesitation.

## Custom Fit. Secure Design.

Each restoration begins with a comprehensive plan. After placing a series of implants, we design your custom denture to attach securely to them—achieving a precise fit and natural aesthetic.

Implant-supported dentures can be:

* ◆ **Fixed** – attached permanently to implants for maximum security
* ◆ **Removable by the dentist** – cleaned or adjusted as needed
* ◆ Crafted from high-quality materials for lifelike translucency and durability

In some cases, this treatment may be part of a [**Full Mouth Reconstruction**](/services/restorative-dentistry/full-mouth-reconstruction) to restore overall function and appearance. Implant dentures can also be combined with other services, including [**Dental Implants**](/services/restorative-dentistry/dental-implants) and [**Dental Crowns**](/services/restorative-dentistry/dental-crowns) when needed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are implant-supported dentures comfortable?


Yes. They’re designed to feel secure and natural, with none of the movement or irritation often associated with traditional dentures.

### How many implants are needed?


Typically, 4 to 6 implants per arch are used to support a full denture. Your exact treatment plan will be tailored to your anatomy and needs.

### Can I eat normally with them?


Absolutely. These restorations restore near-full bite function, allowing you to enjoy a wider range of foods without discomfort or instability.

### Is this the same as All-on-4?


All-on-4 is a type of implant-supported denture. We offer several configurations and will determine which is right for you during your consultation.
