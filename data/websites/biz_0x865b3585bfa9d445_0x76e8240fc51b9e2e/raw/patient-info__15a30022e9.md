GUIDED WELCOME

## What to Expect as a New Patient

Every new patient experience is customized, but the process is always guided, clear, and rooted in respect. Whether you’re joining us for [cosmetic dentistry](/services/cosmetic-dentistry), [TMJ therapy](/services/general-dentistry/tmj-therapy), or general wellness care, our concierge-style approach ensures you never feel rushed or overlooked.

“We value your time and trust. Our goal is to honor both.”

### Your First Visit May Include

01

A conversation—not just a chart review—about your goals, past dental experiences, and expectations.

02

A full set of [digital X-rays](/services/general-dentistry/dental-technology) or scans to evaluate your dental and skeletal health.

03

A comprehensive exam with [Dr. Kevin Winters](/about/meet-the-doctor), who will walk you through every finding and option with clarity.

04

Discussion of timelines, fees, and financing—always tailored to your pace.

TRANSPARENT VALUE

## Financing & Insurance

01

### Fee-for-Service

**The Hills Dental Spa is a 100% fee-for-service practice.** This means we are not in-network with any dental insurance providers. Instead, we focus solely on what’s best for your care, without limitations or outside influence.

02

### Out-of-Network

Many of our patients choose to use out-of-network benefits by filing with their insurance provider directly.

03

### Financing Options

We offer third-party financing options through:

* CareCredit
* Cherry
* Proceed Finance

04

### Pre-Qualification

Our team will assist with pre-qualification and help you find the best fit for your treatment and financial goals.
