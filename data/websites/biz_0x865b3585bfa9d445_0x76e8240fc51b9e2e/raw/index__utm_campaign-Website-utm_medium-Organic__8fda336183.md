## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is TMJ therapy surgical?


No. Our TMJ work focuses entirely on non-surgical, physiological systems guided by advanced, computer-based diagnostics to locate and resolve the exact origin of neuromuscular tension.

### What is a fee-for-service practice?


It means we operate independently of insurance-driven limits. We are not restricted in the time we spend with you, the quality of dental materials we use, or the advanced procedures we recommend. You receive pure, uncompromised care.

### Are financing arrangements available?


Yes. While we are a private, fee-for-service clinic, we partner with leading premium medical finance providers including CareCredit, Cherry, and Proceed Finance to make your treatment plan comfortable and seamless.

### Where is the practice located?


Our private atelier is located in Austin, TX, and we proudly serve clients from West Lake Hills, Rollingwood, Lost Creek, Lakeway, and beyond who seek a higher standard of care.
