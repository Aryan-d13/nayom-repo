# Snore Guards in Austin, TX

Better Rest for You—and Everyone Around You

Snoring isn’t always just a nuisance—it can be a sign of airway obstruction that disrupts your rest and affects your quality of life. At [**The Hills Dental Spa**](/about), we offer custom snore guards designed to open the airway and minimize nighttime noise, helping you (and your partner) wake up feeling more restored.

It’s a small device with a big impact.

## What Are Snore Guards?

Snore guards are custom-fitted oral appliances that gently reposition the lower jaw or tongue to keep the airway open during sleep. They’re lightweight, comfortable, and easy to wear—especially compared to bulky CPAP alternatives.

These appliances are ideal for:

* ◆ Mild to moderate snoring
* ◆ Patients with airway constriction that worsens during sleep
* ◆ Individuals who don’t tolerate CPAP
* ◆ Bed partners affected by chronic snoring
* ◆ Support alongside [**Sleep Apnea Treatment**](/services/general-dentistry/sleep-apnea-treatment)

The design of each snore guard is based on your anatomy, bite, and sleep concerns, offering a personalized solution that fits into your nighttime routine.

## A Quieter, Healthier Night’s Sleep

Snore guards don’t just reduce noise—they improve breathing, enhance sleep quality, and reduce the fatigue often caused by poor airflow. By preventing soft tissues from collapsing into the airway, these devices promote a more restful and oxygen-rich night’s sleep.

We provide custom snore guards for patients in Austin, West Lake Hills, Rollingwood, Lost Creek, and Lakeway, TX, helping individuals and couples find a quieter, more restorative way forward.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will a snore guard cure my snoring?


While no device guarantees a full cure, snore guards are highly effective at reducing or eliminating snoring caused by airway obstruction.

### Is it uncomfortable to sleep with?


Most patients adjust within a few nights. Our custom appliances are slim, non-intrusive, and tailored to your anatomy.

### How is this different from a store-bought mouthpiece?


Custom snore guards are professionally designed for your unique bite and airway—offering better comfort, safety, and effectiveness than over-the-counter options.

### Can a snore guard help with sleep apnea?


Yes, in some cases. For mild to moderate obstructive sleep apnea, snore guards can be part of a broader [**Sleep Apnea Treatment**](/services/general-dentistry/sleep-apnea-treatment) strategy.
