# Smile Makeovers in Austin, TX

A New Smile. A New Way of Living.

A smile makeover at [**The Hills Dental Spa**](/about) is not about perfection. It’s about alignment—between who you are and how you show up in the world. ## What Is a Smile Makeover?

A smile makeover is a tailored combination of treatments used to address multiple aesthetic or functional concerns. No two are the same. Each one is built around your goals, facial features, and how your teeth interact with your expression, speech, and confidence.

Your makeover may include:

* ◆ [**Porcelain Veneers**](/services/cosmetic-dentistry/porcelain-veneers) to create uniformity and symmetry
* ◆ [**Prepless Dental Veneers**](/services/cosmetic-dentistry/prepless-dental-veneers) for a more conservative refinement
* ◆ [**Teeth Whitening**](/services/cosmetic-dentistry/teeth-whitening) for a brighter, more youthful appearance
* ◆ [**Dental Bonding**](/services/cosmetic-dentistry/dental-bonding) to smooth and correct minor flaws
* ◆ [**Invisalign**](/services/cosmetic-dentistry/invisalign) or [**Clear Aligners**](/services/cosmetic-dentistry/clear-aligners) to discreetly straighten teeth
* ◆ [**Gummy Smile Treatment**](/services/cosmetic-dentistry/gummy-smile-treatment) to improve tooth-to-gum balance

We may also address underlying issues such as bite alignment or tooth wear that could impact both aesthetics and long-term function.

## A Personal, Artistic Process

Each smile makeover begins with a conversation—quiet, thoughtful, and unhurried. We look beyond your teeth and into how your smile fits into your overall presence. Skin tone, lip shape, eye placement, posture, and lifestyle are all considered in the design.

Treatments are staged and sequenced for both comfort and continuity. Nothing is rushed. Every detail is intentional. The goal is to create a smile that feels lived-in, not manufactured—one that reflects the way you already carry yourself, just with more ease.

The entire process is guided by [**Dr. Kevin Winters**](/about/meet-the-doctor), whose advanced training in cosmetic and neuromuscular dentistry ensures that every change supports both beauty and balance. We provide comprehensive smile makeovers to individuals in Austin, West Lake Hills, Rollingwood, Lost Creek, and Lakeway, TX.

## The Impact of Subtle Confidence

A smile makeover isn’t always about dramatic change. Often, it’s a series of subtle shifts that create space for bigger changes in how you engage with life. It’s what happens when you stop worrying about how your smile looks and start focusing on where it can take you.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What’s included in a smile makeover?


Each makeover is personalized, but it may involve a combination of veneers, whitening, bonding, clear aligners, or other cosmetic procedures. We design the plan based on your goals and needs.

### How long does a smile makeover take?


The timeline varies depending on the treatments selected. Some patients complete their makeover in a few weeks, while others spread out treatment over several months for comfort and convenience.

### Is a smile makeover only about looks?


No. While aesthetics are central, we also consider how your teeth function, how your bite aligns, and how the changes will support your long-term oral health.

### Will people know I’ve had dental work done?


Not unless you tell them. The goal is natural-looking results that complement your features—not something that looks artificial or obvious.
