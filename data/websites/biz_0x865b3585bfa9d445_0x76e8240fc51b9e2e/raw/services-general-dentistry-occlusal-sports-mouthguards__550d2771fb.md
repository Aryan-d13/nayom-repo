## Designed for Precision and Comfort

There’s no one-size-fits-all when it comes to protecting your teeth. Our custom mouthguards are created from precise digital impressions, ensuring a secure, lightweight fit that supports function without distraction.

We provide two primary types:

* ◆ **Occlusal Mouthguards** – Worn during sleep to protect teeth from grinding and clenching (bruxism). These guards can also help manage tension in the jaw and reduce discomfort associated with misaligned bites.
* ◆ **Sports Mouthguards** – Designed for athletes of all ages, these guards cushion impact during high-contact activities, reducing the risk of dental trauma, fractures, or soft tissue injuries.

Each guard is fabricated to offer durability, breathability, and a barely-there feel that allows you to focus on movement or rest—not your appliance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between an occlusal guard and a sports mouthguard?


Occlusal guards protect against grinding during sleep. Sports mouthguards absorb physical impact during athletic activity. Both are customized to your needs.

### Can I buy a mouthguard at the drugstore instead?


Over-the-counter guards often lack the fit and effectiveness of custom versions. Ill-fitting guards can be uncomfortable or even harmful over time.

### How long does a custom mouthguard last?


With proper care, most mouthguards last several years. However, changes in bite or wear may require periodic replacement or adjustments.

### Is an occlusal guard the same as a night guard?


Yes. Both terms describe guards worn during sleep to protect teeth from nighttime grinding and clenching.
