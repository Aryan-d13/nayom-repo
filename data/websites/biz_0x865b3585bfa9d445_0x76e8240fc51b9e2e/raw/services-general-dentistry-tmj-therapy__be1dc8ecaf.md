## What Is TMJ Disorder?

TMJ disorder (or TMD) occurs when the temporomandibular joints—located on each side of the jaw—stop functioning harmoniously. It can cause pain, limited jaw movement, and symptoms that radiate well beyond the mouth.

You may be experiencing TMJ dysfunction if you suffer from:

* ◆ Jaw pain or tightness
* ◆ Headaches or migraines
* ◆ Clicking or popping sounds when opening your mouth
* ◆ Facial tension or pain behind the eyes
* ◆ Neck, shoulder, or upper back discomfort
* ◆ Difficulty chewing or opening your mouth fully

Because TMJ symptoms overlap with so many other conditions, diagnosis and treatment require expertise—and the right tools.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is this treatment surgical?


No. Our TMJ therapy is completely non-surgical and focuses on restoring balance through bite alignment, muscle relaxation, and supportive orthotic devices.

### How long does it take to see results?


Many patients experience relief within weeks. Chronic or complex cases may take longer, depending on the severity and treatment approach.

### What makes your TMJ therapy different?


We use advanced computer-based diagnostics to guide therapy with precision. Every decision is backed by data—not guesswork—and treatment is completely customized.

### Can this help with migraines or neck pain?


Yes. TMJ dysfunction often creates a ripple effect, contributing to head, neck, and even shoulder pain. By treating the root cause, many patients experience significant relief in these areas.
