# About The Hills Dental Spa

A Spa-Like Dental Experience, Designed for How You Want to Feel

PERSONAL CARE REIMAGINED

**The Hills Dental Spa** was never meant to feel like a typical dental office—because we don’t believe your care should feel typical. Here, every detail is curated to deliver a uniquely personal, restorative, and elevated experience. From the moment you arrive, you’ll sense the difference: a quieter space, a warmer welcome, and a practice built entirely around your comfort and goals.

Patients from Austin, West Lake Hills, Rollingwood, Lost Creek, and Lakeway, TX trust us not only with their smiles—but with their stories, their time, and their long-term health.

## Meet Dr. Kevin Winters

Program Director, LVI Global

Guiding each experience is **Dr. Kevin Winters**, whose work has influenced thousands of clinicians globally in the fields of aesthetic and neuromuscular dentistry. His approach is deliberate, exacting, and quietly obsessive in its pursuit of precision—where function and beauty are never treated as separate ideas.

Dr. Winters is not just a practitioner; he is an educator. As a featured speaker and clinical instructor at the Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Studies (LVI), he has spent decades teaching other dentists the art and science of smile design and full-mouth reconstruction.

At The Hills Dental Spa, he brings this level of world-class expertise to a private, boutique setting, ensuring that every crown, veneer, and treatment plan is executed with master-level precision.
