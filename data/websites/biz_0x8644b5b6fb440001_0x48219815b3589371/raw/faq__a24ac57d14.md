Yes, we reserve a portion of our restaurant to accommodate walk-ins on a first-come, first-serve basis.

During peak hours our wait time can average from fifteen mins to an hour and a half. We encourage you to check in with our host stand who can give you an updated wait time when you arrive. Once you’ve been added to our waitlist you can enjoy cocktails in our wait area or explore the neighborhood and we will text you when your table is ready!

Our booking window opens up 30 days in advance at midnight via Resy. Larger parties of 10 or more guests can be reserved up to three months in advance.

Yes, we have a small waiting area with cocktail service.

We love dogs but only ADA service animals trained to perform a specific task to assist a person with a disability are allowed in the restaurant and on our patio.

Yes. We allow outside desserts with a cake cutting fee of $25

Our liquor license does not allow outside beverages but we have a full bar which offers a carefully curated agave spirit selection, signature cocktails, and natural wines.

You can make a reservation for up to 9 guests in our main dining room and up to 6 guests on our patio. For reservations of 10 or more guests, an “omakase” dining experience in our private dining room is available. A minimum cost spent and non-refundable deposit is required to reserve this space. There is a food and beverage minimum and non-refundable deposit is required to reserve this space.

Yes, at this time this is our only offering for parties of 10 or more. We are dedicated to providing you with exceptional dining for any celebration and believe our semi-private dining room offers the level of hospitality that best accommodates large groups. We are happy to work with you and your guests to curate a unique experience!

If you are looking for an experience other than a semi private dining room, please check our online availability to book a standard reservation.

At this time we do not offer valet service. We provide limited onsite parking and offer partial validation at the LAZ parking garage (1801 E 6th Street), located directly across the street from Suerte.

There is no dress code.

Yes, we offer online catering orders for pickup or drop-off through a third-party delivery service, as well as full-service catering. Both options are subject to availability! For more information, please click [HERE](https://www.suerteatx.com/catering/).

We are happy to accommodate most dietary restrictions and aversions, but there are some limitations due to the nature of our menu. If any of your guests have requests for accommodations, please let us know prior to confirming your reservation.
