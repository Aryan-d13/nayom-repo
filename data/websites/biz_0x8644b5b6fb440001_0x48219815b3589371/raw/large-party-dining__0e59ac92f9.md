# Large Party Dining

*Tiempo de celebrar?* Suerte offers a variety of spaces that can accommodate larger groups with a seasonally curated menu by Chef Fermin Nunez. For parties of 10 or more, please fill out the form below to further inquire about our large party dining experience!

 To ensure we create the perfect experience for you, please give us at least one week’s notice. All requests are considered on a first come, first serve basis. Thank you for your understanding, and we can’t wait to celebrate with you!

* ## Semi-Private Dining Room

  Our semi-private dining room can accommodate up to 20 guests at a single table or up to 24 guests at 3 adjacent tables.
  For additional privacy, the room can be closed off from our main dining room by a sliding fabric partition for groups of 24 or less.

  **Capacity:** 24 guests
* ## Outdoor Covered Deck

  This covered outdoor space overlooks historic 6th street and is ideal for seated and standing events.

  The deck has two built-in banquets that can accommodate up to 20 guests each for seated events and floor plans are available to accommodate standing cocktail events. The deck is equipped with retractable shades, fans, and misters.

  **Capacity:** 36 seated// 50 standing
* ## Full Restaurant Buyout

  For those looking for exclusive access to the entire restaurant, we offer full buyouts that can be curated to your needs, your guests, and your event. This is a great option for larger groups looking for a private experience. Guests will have access to every restaurant space including our covered deck, E 6th patio and cocktail and bar area.
  We offer a variety of table arrangements to accommodate your style of event. Our events team can help coordinate AV, music, and decor. Custom a la carte experiences can be added such as cooking classes, mezcal tastings, and beverage pairing packages as requested.

  **Capacity:** 100 seated// 100 standing
