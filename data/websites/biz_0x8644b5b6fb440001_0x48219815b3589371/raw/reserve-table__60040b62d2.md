# Reserve a Table

Reserve a Table

We accept online reservations of up to 9 guests for dinner and weekend brunch. Reservations are released up to 30 days in advance through OpenTable for both our main dining room and covered patio. Patio reservations are weather permitting and are subject to change.

*We do not allow pets in any areas of our restaurant including our outdoor seating areas.*

Reservations will be held for 15 minutes past the stated time before being released. Special requests such as highchairs, strollers, or additional seating preferences should be noted at the time of booking and we will accommodate to the best of our ability.

*All available reservations are listed online but we always save a portion of our seating for walk-in guests at our bar, in our dining room, and outdoors. We ask that you check in with our hosts in person to add your name to our waitlist.*

***We provide limited onsite parking and offer partial validation at the LAZ parking garage (1801 E 6th Street), located directly across the street from Suerte.***

## Large Party Reservations

For parties of 10 or more, we offer a large party dining experience in our semi-private dining room or outdoor covered deck. Please complete the inquiry form below and our reservations team will reach out promptly.

All inquiries are answered internally in the order they are received within 24 to 48 hours.
