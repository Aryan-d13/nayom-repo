Smile makeovers have become a popular service over the years, even if they have a tendency to be confused with cosmetic treatments. Here, we’ll look at the differences between the two, so it’s easier to decide which one is best for you.

### What Is a Smile Makeover?

A smile makeover typically refers to a list of treatments that can restore your oral health and appearance. While it doesn’t refer to a specific treatment plan, the idea is for a [dentist in Fort Lauderdale, FL](https://floridasmilesdental.com) to fix any underlying structural issues before undertaking the aesthetic changes you want to make.

A smile makeover might entail:

* **Cleanings**: Removing all plaque and tartar from the teeth and gums. This may include a periodontic cleaning, where the dentist will go under the gum line to remove buildup.
* **Restorative dentistry:** Implants, bridges, fillings, and crowns are all potential options for a dentist to restore your smile.
* **Orthodontia**: A dentist may refer you to an orthodontist who can help you straighten your teeth with either braces or clear aligners.
* **Cosmetic services:** Veneers, teeth whitening, gumline contouring, and bondings can cover up any number of flaws in your smile for the perfect finish.

You can think of a smile makeover as a full undertaking if you want to improve your oral health, while [cosmetic treatments in Fort Lauderdale, FL](https://floridasmilesdental.com/services/cosmetic-dentistry/) focus largely on giving you those movie star whites.
