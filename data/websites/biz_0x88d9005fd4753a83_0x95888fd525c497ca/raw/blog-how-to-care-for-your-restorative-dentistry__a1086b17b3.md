Restorative dental work can refer to anything from a single small filling to a full set of implants. The good news is that the general advice to care for your restorative dental work is neither groundbreaking or complicated. We’ll look at the basics and give you a few additional tips if you want to get the best returns on your investment.

### Keep Up with Everyday Dental Care

[Restorative dental work in Lighthouse Point, FL](https://floridasmilesdental.com/services/restorative-dentistry/), like crowns and artificial teeth, won’t suffer from tooth decay the way that natural teeth will. However, you’ll still need to brush and floss like normal to keep bacteria and plaque away from your gum and jawbone. The more consistent you are with hygiene, including mouthwash and water flossers, the more likely you are to avoid further oral decay in the future.

### Go Easy

While it may not be a crime to enjoy a cigarette, hard candy, or crusty piece of bread, you can put dental work in danger if you’re constantly subjecting it to poison or pressure. Most artificial dental work can handle quite a bit of strain, so there’s no reason to switch to soft foods altogether (after all, that’s probably why you got the dental work to begin with). Still, you may want to cut down on bad habits and limit your crunchiest or stickiest indulgences.

### See a Dentist in Lighthouse Point

Just as you did with your natural teeth, you’ll want to see a [dentist in Lighthouse Point, FL](https://floridasmilesdental.com) every six months to evaluate your oral health and remove any plaque or tartar that you couldn’t reach on your own.
