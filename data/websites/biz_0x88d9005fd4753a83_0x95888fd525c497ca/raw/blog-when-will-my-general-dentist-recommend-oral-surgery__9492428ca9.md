### When Will Dentists Recommend Surgery?

Dentists will typically recommend surgery for the following reasons:

* **Dental implants:** As one of the most common surgical procedures, dental implants require dentists to cut under the gumline to implant the metal post.
* **Extractions**: Surgical tooth extractions typically refer to removing impacted teeth. The dentist will need to cut below the gum to fully remove the tooth. This is unlike a simple tooth extraction, which doesn’t require surgery.
* **Grafting**: Grafting is a surgical process to restore gums or bone, and can be used to fix anything from general recession to jawbone deterioration. A general dentist may recommend this to prepare for another surgery. For example, grafting gum tissue before implants.
* **Tongue-ties**: A surgery known as a frenectomy can remove or reshape tissues that attach the tongue to the mouth and restrict tongue movement.
* **Sleep apnea**: Sleep apnea can be linked to excessive tissues in the throat, and a surgical process may be able to remove the obstructions so you can sleep easier.
* **Jaw repositioning**: If your jaw can’t be repositioned by orthodontics, a dentist may recommend [oral surgery in Lighthouse Point, FL](https://floridasmilesdental.com/services/oral-surgery/) to correct the issue.
