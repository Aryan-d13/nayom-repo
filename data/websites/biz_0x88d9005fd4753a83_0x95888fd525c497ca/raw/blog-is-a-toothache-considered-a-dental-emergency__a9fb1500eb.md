### My Tooth Hurts, What Should I Do?

Toothaches can be caused by anything from stray seeds to mild gum infections. If you don’t have accompanying symptoms, like a fever or blood, the first thing to do is brush and floss to see if that resolves the problem. It could be as simple as dislodging a foreign object and giving your mouth a day or two to heal.

If your pain persists, the next step is to assess your discomfort. If your pain is relatively mild, you don’t necessarily need to call an emergency care line. If it’s more moderate to severe, though, we recommend discussing your symptoms with an on-call dentist or specialist.

### When Is It an Emergency?

The biggest indicator of emergency toothache is a fever. Fevers can indicate infection, which can unfortunately be a fatal disorder. (If the infection isn’t caught, it can move to your bloodstream and through the rest of the body.) However, even if you don’t have a fever, severe pain of any kind is considered an urgent case for [dentists in Fort Lauderdale, FL](https://floridasmilesdental.com).
