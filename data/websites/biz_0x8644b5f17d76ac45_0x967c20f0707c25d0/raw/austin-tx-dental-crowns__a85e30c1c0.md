## Repair the strength of your teeth

A dental crown is a protective cap that covers the visible part of a tooth, preserving its structure and restoring its original appearance. Porcelain crowns may be necessary for various reasons, such as severe tooth decay, fractured teeth, or as part of a root canal or dental implant procedure.

At TRU Dentistry Austin, we specialize in crafting custom porcelain crowns that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth. Our advanced technology enables us to provide same-day crown options to suit your busy schedule.

### My crown is damaged, can you assist me?

We understand the importance of a strong and secure crown to protect your tooth and maintain your oral health. Our team of experienced professionals will carefully assess your situation and provide you with the best solution to ensure your crown is restored to its optimal condition. Whether you need a simple repair or a completely new crown, we have the knowledge and expertise to meet your needs. ### Getting A Crown In Just Two Visits At TRU Dentistry

Our team is dedicated to ensuring your comfort and providing a personalized experience. We use biocompatible materials and gentle, minimally-invasive techniques for all our crowns.

During the 2-week period while your final crown is being made, we will provide you with a temporary crown that is comfortable for your bite and does not cause any discomfort to your tongue. The temporary crown is meant to protect your tooth until the final crown is ready.
