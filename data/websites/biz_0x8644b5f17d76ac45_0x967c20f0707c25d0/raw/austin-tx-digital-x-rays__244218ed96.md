## Digital Radiography Enhances Dentistry

The use of digital radiography in dentistry has revolutionized the field, offering advanced diagnostic tools that enhance oral healthcare. With crystal-clear scans of a patient's jawbone, teeth, and supporting structures, we gain a comprehensive understanding of their oral health. These scans enable us to identify dental restorations, potential issues, and accurately diagnose problems, allowing us to discuss treatment options with patients. Digital X-rays provide unparalleled accuracy and precision, are environmentally friendly, reduce radiation exposure, and allow for easy storage and sharing of patient records. This technology transforms dentistry, ensuring the highest standard of care for patients' oral health and overall well-being.

### You will receive exactly what you perceive.

Capturing digital X-rays is simple and exposes patients to up to 85% less radiation. By placing a small sensor in the mouth, clear images can be instantly viewed. This allows us to pinpoint areas of concern and provide feedback to improve at-home hygiene and ongoing care. Digital files can also be easily stored, shared with insurance providers or dental specialists, and compared over time to monitor changes in oral health.

### Benefits of Digital X-rays

Digital radiography provides numerous advantages over traditional dental x-rays. Firstly, it offers a faster process as the sensor can be swiftly moved from tooth to tooth, and the x-ray image can be instantly viewed on a computer monitor. This eliminates the need to wait for chemical development, as required with film packets.

Moreover, digital x-rays are environmentally friendly and safer for patients due to the absence of chemicals in the development process and up to 80% less x-ray radiation compared to conventional x-rays.

Additionally, digital x-ray technology allows for more efficient diagnosis and treatment. The clear and detailed images provided by digital x-rays enable easy visibility and the ability to make adjustments and examine details. These images can also be stored electronically and easily shared with specialists and insurance companies via email, making the process more convenient and streamlined.

In conclusion, digital radiography offers speed, safety, enhanced viewing capabilities, and easy sharing of images, making it a superior option to traditional dental x-rays.
