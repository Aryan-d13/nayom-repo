## The TRU Dentistry Idea

TRU Dentistry was founded out of a desire to do something different. It wasn’t just about starting a business or even having a local dentist office. For us, it’s an ideal. We started this brand because we wanted to plant roots in the Austin community and provide [dentistry services](/services/) that are honest, fair, kind and TRU.

We strive for excellence with every patient experience. From the moment our patients walk in our doors, we consider you family and will always treat you as such.

We’re committed to comprehensive communication, personal connections, honest pricing, and an inviting and comfortable atmosphere. Most of all, we’re committed to being the best dentist in TX and providing a TRUly unique dentistry experience.
