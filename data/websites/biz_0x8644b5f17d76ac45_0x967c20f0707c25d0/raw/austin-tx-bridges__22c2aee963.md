## Dental Bridges for Missing Teeth Benefits

A dental bridge is a permanent dental solution for replacing missing teeth. Unlike removable options, the bridge is securely attached to neighboring teeth, eliminating the need for removal and cleaning. Each bridge is custom-made for the patient, ensuring a natural appearance. Typically, fixed bridges can last for many years before needing to be replaced.

### Why Would I Need a Dental Bridge?

If you are missing a tooth and wish to avoid dental implant surgery or are not a candidate for it, getting a dental bridge is an excellent option. A dental bridge can effectively restore the appearance and functionality of your smile, while also preventing neighboring teeth from shifting or becoming overcrowded.

### Who can get a dental bridge?

Our dental team will evaluate your teeth to see if a fixed bridge is the right option for you. The bridge will be placed over two neighboring teeth with precision to ensure a secure fit. Larger bridges may be less stable, so if needed, we may recommend using implants for additional support.

### Dental Bridge Procedure Overview

To start the procedure, we will use a local anesthetic to numb a specific area of your mouth. Next, we will shape and file down the surrounding teeth to make room for the new fixed bridge. Impressions will be taken to create both a temporary and permanent bridge. After the shaping and filing is done, you will leave with the temporary bridge. To get the permanent bridge, you will need to schedule a second appointment. During this appointment, the temporary bridge will be removed and the permanent one will be securely placed with dental adhesive. We will then polish the bridge and make any necessary adjustments. You will also receive instructions on how to care for the bridge through brushing and flossing to ensure its longevity.
