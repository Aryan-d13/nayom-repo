[Equality](https://www.trudentistryaustin.com/supporting-equality-in-oral-healthcare/) is a cause that’s near and dear to us, which is why we’re thrilled to announce that TRU Dentistry Austin is partnering with the [Equality Alliance](https://www.unitethefightgala.com/about-the-equality-alliance-1) for their annual [Unite the Fight Gala](https://www.unitethefightgala.com/).

The event takes place on Saturday, September 21 at 6:30 PM. Join us at the Hilton DT ATX as we join with several local LGBT charities to collaborate for one common end goal: equality.

In addition to supporting a great cause, there will be entertainment, food, drinks, a silent auction, and even the chance to win a car from [Mercedes-Benz of Austin](https://www.unitethefightgala.com/mercedesbenzofaustinraffle)!

You can purchase your [tickets here](https://www.unitethefightgala.com/tickets). We hope to see you there to have a great time and support a worthy cause!
