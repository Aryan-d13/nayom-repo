Many people believe they can’t obtain quality and much needed dental care without [insurance](https://www.trudentistryaustin.com/insurance/). That simply isn’t true. TRU Dentistry Austin wants everyone to know what is true. They have made it their mission to serve patients with or without insurance by providing quality dentistry through a variety of payment options.

“We understand that some patients may not have an employer that offers insurance, or simply can’t afford it. Our number one goal is to serve anyone and everyone, regardless of their financial situation,” says TRU Dentistry owner,  “I can’t tell you how many patients have come in and said they can’t take care of certain problems until they get their own insurance. We want patients to know that not having insurance is absolutely fine.”

### Dental Insurance vs. TRU Club

Dental insurance can be extremely beneficial, and if an employer offers it, it’s a wonderful benefit to take advantage of. However, if that’s not an option for you, you do not have to go without care or obtain your own plan in order to maintain your oral health. The TRU Dentistry team created the [TRU Club](https://www.trudentistryaustin.com/insurance/) so patients without insurance can get the care they need at an affordable price.

Unlike insurance, the TRU Club has no annual premiums, no waiting period, no deductibles, no maximums and will never requite patients to submit a claim. TRU Club members also receive all services at a discounted price.

“The dental industry has been lacking in emphasizing the importance of dental hygiene. It’s not about sexy teeth. It should be about feeling good and having a healthy smile, no matter your situation. For this reason, we are currently waiving the fee for TRU Club,”.
