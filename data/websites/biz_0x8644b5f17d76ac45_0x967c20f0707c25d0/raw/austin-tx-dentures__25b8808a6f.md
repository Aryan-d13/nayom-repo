## Repair the strength of your teeth

If you have lost several teeth due to decay, injury, or aging, dentures could be the ideal solution for you. Today's dentures are crafted from strong materials and are easy to maintain. When custom-made and properly fitted, dentures can mimic the look and function of natural teeth, allowing you to smile, talk, and eat with ease.

That being said, there are other options available for replacing missing teeth. We are here to assist you in exploring various treatments and offering all the necessary details to help you choose the most suitable option for your individual requirements.

### Treatment Options At TRU Dentistry Austin

[Image: Image]

### Traditional Dentures

Traditional dentures are a cost-effective option for replacing multiple missing teeth. They can replace all the teeth on either the upper or lower jaw and are custom-made to fit your gum line perfectly.

[Image: Image]

### Overdentures

Overdentures, also known as implant-supported dentures, rely on nearby natural teeth or dental implants for stability. This type of restoration provides a strong and long-lasting solution, while also enhancing the strength of your jawbone.

[Image: Image]

### Partial Dentures

A partial denture can be either removable or fixed permanently, such as an overdenture. This restoration relies on your natural teeth for support and offers benefits like simple cleaning, upkeep, and improved aesthetics.
