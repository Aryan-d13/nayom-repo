## Persistent Tooth Sensitivity

Sensitivity to hot, cold, or sweet foods is often an early indicator of enamel wear or tooth decay. While occasional sensitivity may not be serious, frequent discomfort can signal a deeper issue that requires professional attention.Ignoring sensitivity may allow minor damage to progress into cavities or infections. Routine dental work can identify the root cause and provide targeted treatment before symptoms worsen.

## Bleeding or Swollen Gums

Healthy gums should not bleed during brushing or flossing. Swelling, redness, or tenderness can point to early-stage gum disease.Common signs include:

* Bleeding when brushing or flossing
* Gum tenderness or puffiness
* Persistent bad breath

Addressing these symptoms early helps prevent advanced periodontal disease, which can lead to tooth loss and more complex procedures.

## Lingering Tooth Pain

Tooth pain that does not go away should never be ignored. Even mild discomfort can indicate decay, infection, or damage beneath the surface.Pain may present as:

* A dull, constant ache
* Sharp pain when biting down
* Intermittent discomfort that returns over time

Seeking routine dental work allows for early diagnosis and treatment, helping to prevent a dental emergency that may require urgent care.

## Changes in Tooth Appearance

Visible changes in teeth often signal underlying issues. Discoloration, cracks, or rough edges may indicate decay or structural damage.Early evaluation can help determine whether restorative treatments such as fillings or bonding are needed. Addressing these concerns promptly supports both function and appearance.

## Jaw Discomfort or Headaches

Frequent jaw pain or headaches can be linked to dental problems such as teeth grinding or misalignment. These issues may worsen over time if left untreated.A dental evaluation can identify contributing factors and provide solutions to relieve discomfort while protecting the teeth from further wear.
