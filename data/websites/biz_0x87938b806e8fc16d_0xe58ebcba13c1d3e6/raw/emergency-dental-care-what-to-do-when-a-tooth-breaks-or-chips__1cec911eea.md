## Steps to Take Right Away

When a tooth breaks or chips, knowing what to do in the moment can significantly impact the outcome. Take these steps until you can see a dentist:

* Rinse your mouth gently with warm water to keep the area clean and prevent irritation.
* Apply a cold compress to the outside of your face to reduce swelling.
* Save any tooth fragments and bring them with you to your appointment.
* For a tooth that has been knocked out, moisture is key—return it carefully to its socket if possible, or place it in milk to protect it before treatment.
* Avoid chewing on the injured side to prevent additional damage.

These quick actions can provide temporary relief while protecting the tooth until professional care is available.

## Why Immediate Care Matters

Putting off care for a broken tooth can result in serious health issues beyond appearance. Without prompt care, the damage may worsen and expose the tooth to infection or decay. Pain often increases over time, and small chips can turn into larger cracks that require more extensive treatment.

Seeking emergency dental care right away ensures that problems are addressed before they progress, reducing the risk of permanent damage and helping you get back to eating, speaking, and smiling with confidence.

## Treatment Options for Broken or Chipped Teeth

Dentists use different methods to restore chipped or broken teeth, tailoring treatment to the level of damage. Common treatments may include:

* Dental bonding to repair small chips and restore shape
* Crowns to cover and protect larger fractures
* Veneers for cosmetic repair on front teeth
* Root canal therapy if the pulp of the tooth has been exposed

These treatments are designed to relieve pain, restore function, and improve appearance, helping patients regain a healthy smile.
