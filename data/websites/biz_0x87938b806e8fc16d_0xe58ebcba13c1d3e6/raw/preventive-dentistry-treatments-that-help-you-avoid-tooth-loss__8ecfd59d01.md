## Common Causes Of Tooth Loss

Tooth loss rarely happens overnight. It is usually the result of ongoing oral health issues that go untreated. Some of the most common causes include:

* Gum disease that damages the bone and tissue supporting the teeth
* Untreated cavities that weaken tooth structure
* Poor oral hygiene habits at home
* Infrequent dental visits that allow problems to progress

Addressing these issues early plays a major role in preventing long-term tooth loss.

## Preventive Dentistry Treatments That Protect Your Smile

Several preventive treatments work together to reduce the risk of tooth loss and support a healthier mouth.

* Professional cleanings target plaque and tartar accumulation that regular brushing and flossing may miss
* Routine dental exams and X-rays help detect cavities, bone loss, and other issues early
* Gum disease screenings allow for timely treatment before damage becomes severe
* Fluoride treatments strengthen enamel and help prevent decay
* Personalized oral hygiene guidance improves daily care at home

When used consistently, these services help maintain oral health and reduce the likelihood of tooth loss.
