# Parking

There is a small paved parking lot in front of our bar building (on Chicon), and a larger paved parking lot behind the bar building (also on Chicon).

Street parking is available along Chicon & on nearby streets, 20th & Poquito.

We ask that you do not park in our alleyway, as it obstructs our neighbor’s ability to get to their homes. Thank you!
