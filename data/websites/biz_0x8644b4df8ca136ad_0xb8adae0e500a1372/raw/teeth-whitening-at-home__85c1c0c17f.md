## How Custom Whitening Trays Work

The process is simple: we take precise impressions of your teeth to create custom trays that fit perfectly. These trays are paired with a powerful whitening gel that targets stains and discoloration caused by coffee, tea, wine, tobacco, and more. You’ll wear the trays as instructed—usually for a short period each day—until you reach your desired level of brightness.
