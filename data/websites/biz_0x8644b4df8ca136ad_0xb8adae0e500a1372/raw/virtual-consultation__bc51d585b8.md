It’s the perfect solution for busy professionals, parents, or anyone who values convenience without compromising on quality care.

## Why Virtual Consultations with Breeze?

Virtual consultations are ideal for discussing treatment plans, reviewing X-rays, or determining if you need an in-office visit. At Breeze, we’re committed to making dental care accessible, efficient, and tailored to your lifestyle. Let us help you take the first step toward a healthier smile—all on your schedule.

[Image: breeze logo on computer]

* #### **What can I discuss during a virtual consultation?**

  You can address specific dental concerns, review treatment plans, discuss X-rays, or determine if an in-office visit is necessary.
* #### **How do I schedule a virtual consultation?**

  Simply use our online booking system or app to select a time that works best for you. It’s quick and easy!
* #### **Are VC as effective as in-office visits?**

  While virtual consultations are ideal for initial assessments and discussions, some treatments may still require an in-office visit for hands-on care.
