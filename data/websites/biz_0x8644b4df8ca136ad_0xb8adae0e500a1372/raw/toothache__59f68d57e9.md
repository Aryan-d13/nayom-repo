## How Severe Toothache Works

Toothaches are often caused by issues such as cavities, infections, gum disease, or trauma to the tooth. Severe pain may also indicate deeper problems, such as an abscess or damage to the tooth’s root. Our team uses advanced diagnostic tools to identify the source of your pain and determine the best course of treatment, whether it’s a filling, root canal therapy, or another restorative solution.

* ## What causes a severe toothache?

  Common causes include cavities, tooth decay, infections, gum disease, fractured teeth, or exposed tooth roots.
* ## What should I do if I have a severe toothache?

  Rinse your mouth with warm water, use a cold compress to reduce swelling, and avoid chewing on the affected side. Contact Breeze as soon as possible for professional evaluation and treatment.
* ## Is a severe toothache always an emergency?

  A persistent or severe toothache often indicates an underlying problem that requires prompt attention. If you’re experiencing swelling, fever, or difficulty swallowing, it’s a dental emergency.
