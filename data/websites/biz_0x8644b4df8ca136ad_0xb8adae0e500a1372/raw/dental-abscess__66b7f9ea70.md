## How Dental Abscess Works

A dental abscess typically forms when bacteria infect the pulp inside a tooth, the gum tissue, or the surrounding bone. Common symptoms include severe toothache, swelling in the gums or face, sensitivity to hot or cold, and even fever. Left untreated, the infection can spread to other areas of the body, leading to more serious health concerns.

* ## What causes a dental abscess?

  A dental abscess is typically caused by untreated tooth decay, gum disease, or trauma to the tooth that allows bacteria to enter and infect the pulp or surrounding tissues.
* ## What are the symptoms of a dental abscess?

  Symptoms include severe toothache, swelling, redness in the gums, sensitivity to temperature, bad breath, fever, and sometimes a pus-filled bump near the affected tooth.
* ## Is a dental abscess an emergency?

  Yes, a dental abscess is a dental emergency. Prompt treatment is essential to prevent the infection from spreading and causing further complications.
