## How Jaw Pain Works

Common causes of jaw pain include temporomandibular joint (TMJ) disorders, teeth grinding (bruxism), arthritis, or dental issues like misalignment or impacted teeth. We use a comprehensive approach to diagnose the issue, combining advanced imaging and a thorough evaluation of your symptoms. Treatment may include nightguards, stress management techniques, or restorative care, depending on your specific needs.

* ### What causes jaw pain?

  Jaw pain can result from TMJ disorders, teeth grinding, arthritis, dental misalignment, stress, or trauma to the jaw. A professional evaluation is needed to determine the cause.
* ### How is jaw pain treated?

  Treatment depends on the cause and may include custom nightguards, orthodontic solutions, stress management, or addressing dental issues like impacted teeth.
* ### Is jaw pain related to TMJ disorders?

  Yes, TMJ disorders are a common cause of jaw pain. They occur when the temporomandibular joint (connecting the jaw to the skull) becomes inflamed, misaligned, or damaged.
