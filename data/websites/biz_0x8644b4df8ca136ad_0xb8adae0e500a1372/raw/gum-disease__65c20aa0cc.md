## How Bleeding Gums Work

Gums may bleed due to factors such as plaque buildup, gum disease (gingivitis or periodontitis), aggressive brushing, hormonal changes, or certain medications. While occasional bleeding may not seem alarming, persistent bleeding is often a sign of an underlying issue that requires attention. Our team will evaluate your gum health, recommend the best course of action, and provide tips to improve your oral hygiene routine.

* ### Why do my gums bleed when I brush or floss?

  Bleeding gums can result from plaque buildup, gum disease, or brushing too hard. A proper oral care routine and professional cleaning can help address the issue.
* ### Is bleeding gums a sign of gum disease?

  Yes, bleeding gums are often an early sign of gingivitis, the first stage of gum disease. Left untreated, it can progress to periodontitis, a more severe condition.
* ### How are bleeding gums treated?

  Treatment depends on the cause. Options may include professional cleanings, scaling and root planing, improved oral hygiene, or addressing underlying health conditions.
