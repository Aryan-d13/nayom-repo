## Benefits of Vivera Retainers

* **Custom Fit**
  Designed to fit your teeth perfectly using advanced 3D imaging for optimal comfort and effectiveness.
* **Exceptional Strength**
  Up to 30% stronger than other retainers to prevent teeth from shifting over time.
* **Clear and Discreet**
  Virtually invisible retainers let you maintain your confidence while wearing them.
* **Durable and Long-Lasting**
  Made from high-quality materials for lasting protection against dental movement.

## How Vivera Retainers Work

Vivera® retainers are made using advanced 3D imaging technology, ensuring a precise fit that comfortably holds your teeth in place. Crafted from premium materials, they’re up to 30% stronger than other clear retainers, providing superior protection against shifting. Whether you’ve just completed Invisalign® or other orthodontic treatment, Vivera® retainers are an investment in your long-term smile.
