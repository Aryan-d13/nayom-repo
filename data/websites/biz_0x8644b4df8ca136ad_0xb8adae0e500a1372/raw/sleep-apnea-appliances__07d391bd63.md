## Sleep-Disordered Breathing & Snoring Treatment in Austin

Chronic snoring affects millions of Americans and can disrupt both your sleep and your partner’s rest. At Breeze Dental in Austin, we offer custom-fitted oral appliances specifically designed to reduce or eliminate snoring caused by sleep-disordered breathing. These comfortable devices work by gently repositioning your jaw and tongue to maintain an open airway during sleep, preventing the tissue vibration that causes snoring sounds. During your consultation at our Austin dental office, we conduct a thorough evaluation of your oral structures and discuss your symptoms to determine if a dental sleep appliance is right for you. Many Austin patients experience significant improvement in snoring intensity and frequency after just a few nights of wearing their custom appliance.

## Obstructive Sleep Apnea Solutions | Austin Dental Sleep Medicine

Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA) is a serious condition characterized by repeated breathing interruptions during sleep due to airway blockage. If you’ve been diagnosed with mild to moderate OSA or cannot tolerate CPAP therapy, Breeze Dental offers effective alternative treatment with custom oral appliances. Our Austin sleep dentistry specialists create precision-fitted devices that advance your lower jaw forward during sleep, preventing airway collapse and allowing for normal breathing throughout the night. Unlike CPAP machines, these dental appliances are comfortable, silent, portable, and require no electricity, making them an ideal solution for many Austin sleep apnea patients. We work collaboratively with your sleep physician to ensure your oral appliance therapy is properly monitored and adjusted for maximum effectiveness in managing your OSA symptoms.
