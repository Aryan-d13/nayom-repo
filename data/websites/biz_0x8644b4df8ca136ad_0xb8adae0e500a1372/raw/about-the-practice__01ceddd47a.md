# Not all teeth are the same. And not all dentists are the same.

With a passion for dentistry and a commitment to the highest level of patient care, our team at Breeze Dental is here to provide you with exceptional holistic oral care services tailored to your unique needs.

[Image: Breeze Dental Team]

* ## Comprehensive Care

  A new model of dental care focused on preventing problems, not finding them.
* ## Expert Providers

  Our team is built to collaborate from when you book a session, to when you receive an after-visit health report, and throughout your visit in between.
* ## Cutting-edge Technology

  At Breeze, we use the latest technology to deliver precise and efficient care. We embrace innovation to make your visit faster, more accurate, and tailored to your needs.
* ## Personalized Treatments

  From your initial consultation to the completion of your treatment, our team takes the time to understand your concerns, preferences, and lifestyle. Whether you’re seeking preventive care, cosmetic enhancements, or restorative solutions, we create a treatment plan that’s customized just for you.
* ## Comfortable Environment

  A waiting room you won’t want to leave.
 Instead of reading magazines, you can shop for health and wellness essentials in our retail space. Sure beats sitting anxiously in a chair.
* ## Patient Centered Approach

  Our team is dedicated to providing not just excellent dental care, but an experience that makes you feel heard, valued, and well-informed.
* ## Covered by Insurance

  Insurance can be confusing. At Breeze, it’s likely you’re covered. Because we’re in-network with most dental insurance plans. Call (or text) us to discuss your insurance plan, and how we can offer you the best oral health care possible. We have solutions and memberships for every situation, so you don’t have to worry.
