## How Broken or Fractured Tooth Work

Whether the break is minor or severe, it’s important to address the issue as soon as possible to prevent further complications. A broken tooth can expose the sensitive inner layers, increasing the risk of infection or additional damage. Our team will assess the extent of the break and recommend the best treatment, which may include bonding, a crown, or even root canal therapy in more severe cases.

* ### What should I do if I break or fracture a tooth?

  Rinse your mouth with warm water, apply a cold compress to reduce swelling, and contact Breeze immediately for emergency care. Avoid chewing on the damaged tooth.
* ### Is a broken tooth always a dental emergency?

  Yes, a broken or fractured tooth should be treated promptly to prevent further damage, infection, or pain.
* ### How is a broken tooth repaired?

  Treatment depends on the severity of the break. Options may include bonding, veneers, crowns, or root canal therapy. For severe fractures, extraction may be necessary.
