What Defines Cosmetic Dentistry?

By this point in your life, you've likely already experienced traditional dentistry. Most people go to a general dentist when they get a cavity or toothache. Traditional dentists provide services that are meant to keep your teeth healthy and functioning properly. A standard tooth filling, dental crown, or root canal all fall under the umbrella of traditional dental services. While a cosmetic dental office also offers services that can restore your teeth back to health, the dentist's goals also include making them look as natural as possible. Instead of slapping on a metal crown in your smile zone, our cosmetic dentists will evaluate your smile and find the perfect shade of a restoration to match the rest of your teeth.

Cosmetic Dentistry Services to Consider

Improving the aesthetics of your smile is what cosmetic dental care services are all about. If you need major dental work done, then we have procedures that can stop it from leaving you with a huge gap in your smile. Removing old metal fillings and replacing them with tooth-colored ones can make it less embarrassing to smile when you once had decay in your smile zone. Or, you might enjoy having a teeth-whitening treatment that removes old stains that developed over time. Our compassionate dental team also offers a full range of cosmetic dentistry procedures.

Can You Use Insurance for Affordable Cosmetic Dentistry?

Cosmetic procedures are sometimes considered just that by insurance companies, since many of these are elective. This means that certain procedures might be deemed optional by your insurance company if they don't have an impact on the health of your teeth. That being said, other procedures may fall within your coverage guidelines. The best way to find out is to contact your dental insurance company to find out which parts of your treatment are covered. Many times, you can get some degree of coverage for cosmetic procedures that correct an oral health issue and get your teeth and gums back to a healthy state. We also encourage you to contact our cosmetic dental office for a consultation that gives you the information you need about the recommended treatments that you can discuss with your dental insurance provider.
