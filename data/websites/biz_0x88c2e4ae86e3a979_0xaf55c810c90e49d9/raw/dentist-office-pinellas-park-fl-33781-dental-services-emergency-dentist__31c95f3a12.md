Services Emergency Dental Care Includes

Among the services that emergency dental care includes are all urgent dental care services needed to fix physical damage, alleviate an intense toothache or deal with any other dental problems that may worsen, even to the extent of causing permanent harm, if you don't get treatment as soon as possible.

Florida Dental Centers Pinellas Park emergency dentists in Pinellas Park provide an array of types of emergency dental care near me, from treating sudden injuries to placing whatever temporary dental restorative treatments are needed. Other urgent dental services we offer are described below.

### Fractured Teeth and Broken Teeth

It's vital to get a broken or fractured tooth treated promptly to prevent infection and avoid worse structural damage. Cover any jagged edge with dental wax to prevent cutting your tongue or cheek. Then, call our dental office in Pinellas Park so we can get you in to seek emergency dental care near me as quickly as possible.

### Sensitive Teeth

Minor teeth sensitivity doesn't need urgent attention, but major teeth sensitivity — whether in suddenness or severity — very well might. Sometimes, sensitive teeth can be the result of a gum infection or fractured tooth, both of which would require attention immediately.

### Cavities (aka Dental Decay)

When you have a toothache caused by exposed tooth roots from sizable cavities, or significant dental decay, the dentists in our emergency dental office can repair it and deliver pain relief.

### Crowns Broken or Restorations Lost

More than a cosmetic concern, breaking a crown or losing a restoration can make you more vulnerable to tooth and gum damage. Solutions we can provide at Florida Dental Centers Pinellas Park include temporary and permanent options.

### Lost Fillings or Loose Fillings

When you lose a filling or have a loose filling, your teeth can become sensitive or painful and more vulnerable to harm from injury or disease. The sooner you see a Pinellas Park dentist to replace or restore it, the quicker you can be free of pain and protected from infection or tooth damage.

### Knocked-Out Teeth

If you've had a tooth knocked out, there's a good chance an emergency dentist in Pinellas Park can re-implant it as long as you seek their aid fast enough. In the meantime, rinse the tooth well with warm water, then try to gently place it back into the socket. As you do so, hold it only by the crown. Make sure you find all fragments of the tooth to bring with you to the dentist's office. Call our office for additional guidance.

### Pressure or Pain in the Sinuses

Sinus pressure has a complex relationship with tooth pain. When both symptoms occur simultaneously, ruling out potential causes like wisdom tooth impaction or gum or tooth infection is vital to relieve pain and restore clear and comfortable breathing. Call our emergency dental office if you have sinus pressure or sinus pain at the same time as you have tooth pain.

### Pain and Injury

It isn't uncommon for metal braces to cause orthodontic pain and injury. If you experience such issues, your orthodontic dentist in Pinellas Park can alleviate your symptoms and adjust your braces to avoid any more pain or injury.

### Grinding Teeth Wearing Tooth Enamel

When you grind your teeth (or gnash or clench them) over time, you can wear away at your tooth enamel. Eventually, this can reach the level of a dental emergency, causing extreme pain and sensitivity and exposing the tooth to more severe harm. At an emergency dental office near me, you can have your worn tooth enamel reinforced and night guards created to keep you from grinding your teeth while you sleep.

### Abscessed Tooth

An infection in a tooth's root, or a tooth abscess, can result from gum disease, tooth decay or trauma and cause extreme tooth sensitivity, toothache, halitosis, swollen neck glands, fever, open or draining gum sores or swelling in the gums or face. If untreated, the consequences could be severe.
