# Ammar Sakka, DDS

### General Dentist

Dr. Ammar Sakka was born and grew up in Damascus, Syria. After obtaining his D.D.S. degree in 1995, Dr. Sakka completed a 4-year hospital-based residency earning his Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Specialist degree in 2006. Dr. Sakka owned a private practice for over 6 years in Damascus before moving to Saudi Arabia where he worked as an oral surgeon for more than 9 years. Recently, he obtained his United States Post-Doctoral Certificate in Advanced Dentistry from Nova Southeastern University in Ft. Lauderdale, Florida. Dr. Sakka keeps up to date on the latest dental techniques and technology. In his spare time, he enjoys spending time with his family and friends, traveling, and outdoor activities.

Education:

* 2021 Post Doctoral AEGD, Nova Southeastern University

* 1995 DDS, Al Baath Univ, Syria
