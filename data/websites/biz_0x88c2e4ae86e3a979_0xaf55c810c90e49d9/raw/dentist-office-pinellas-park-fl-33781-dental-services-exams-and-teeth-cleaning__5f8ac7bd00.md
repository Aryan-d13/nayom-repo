Early Gum Disease Treatment Is Essential

In the earliest stages of gum disease, this condition is known as gingivitis and is often reversible with the right treatment. As periodontal disease progresses, however, the damage caused by this dental issue can be serious and could even cause teeth to fall out because of damage to the infrastructure of your gums and jaw. At Florida Dental Centers Pinellas Park, we check your gums at every dental exam to determine whether you are developing gum disease. If you need periodontal treatment, we can offer proven strategies that can keep your teeth and gums healthier.

Treatments for Bruxism

Also known informally as grinding your teeth, bruxism is a dental condition in which the teeth are clenched or grind together during sleep. This unconscious habit can cause broken teeth, cracks in the tooth enamel, and the wearing down of teeth, causing severe discomfort. If you grind your teeth, we can take a mold of your teeth and provide you with custom mouth guard that can protect your teeth from damage caused by this issue. If bruxism is a problem for you or any member of your family, our dental mouthguards can provide protection for your teeth during sleep.

We Offer Teeth Cleaning in Pinellas Park

Dental cleanings offer some important benefits for you and your family members. Some of the most important of these advantages are listed here:

* Lower risk of gum disease: Removing plaque and tartar from your teeth can cut down on irritation of the gums, which is one of the triggering factors that can cause gum disease. Regular dental cleanings can reduce your chance of periodontal disease and can promote better dental health for you now and in the future.
* Fresher breath: Removing food debris, tartar, and plaque from between and behind teeth can eliminate some of the most common causes of bad breath to help you make a great impression on those with whom you come in contact. At Florida Dental Centers Pinellas Park, we recommend twice-annual dental cleanings to reduce the risk of bad breath and the embarrassment this issue can cause.
* Brighter and whiter teeth: Routine dental cleanings also remove tartar, which can cause discolored patches and could affect the appearance of your teeth. By investing in regular cleanings, you can ensure that your teeth look their very best every day.
* Fewer cavities: Finally, regular teeth cleanings can reduce your risk of cavities and tooth decay. This can allow you to bite and chew with greater confidence and can reduce the number of fillings or other treatments required from our office.
