**New Year, New Dental Benefits: Maximize Your Coverage with Emergency Dental of Omaha**

The New Year is here, and it’s the perfect time to focus on fresh starts and healthy habits. One resolution that often goes overlooked is taking care of your oral health. If you have dental insurance, there’s no better time to maximize your new dental benefits and ensure your smile stays bright all year. At Emergency Dental of Omaha, we’re here to help you make the most of your dental coverage so you can kick off the year with a healthy smile and peace of mind.

### **Understanding Your New Dental Benefits**

Every year, most dental insurance plans reset on January 1st. This means your annual benefits, such as deductibles and maximums, start fresh. If you didn’t fully utilize your benefits last year, don’t worry—this is your opportunity to take full advantage of them.

Most insurance plans are designed to encourage preventive care, which is essential for maintaining good oral health and catching potential issues early. Preventive services, like cleanings and exams, are often covered at little to no cost. Beyond that, insurance may also help cover restorative treatments, emergency visits, and more, depending on your specific plan.

Understanding your plan is key to maximizing your benefits. Reviewing your policy details or contacting your insurance provider can clarify what’s covered and what isn’t. If unsure, Emergency Dental of Omaha can help you navigate the fine print to get the most out of your coverage.

---

### **Why Start Early in the Year?**

The beginning of the year is an ideal time to schedule dental appointments. Why? Because it allows you to stay ahead of potential problems and avoid the rush that often comes later in the year when many people scramble to use their remaining benefits.

Scheduling preventive services like cleanings or exams now can help identify minor issues before they become more significant, costly problems. Early use of benefits also spreads care throughout the year, making it easier to prioritize any treatments or follow-up visits your dentist might recommend.

By being proactive, you can protect your oral health and avoid the stress of trying to fit in appointments during the busy holiday season.

---

### **What Services Can Your New Dental Benefits Cover?**

The specific services your insurance covers depend on your plan. However, most dental insurance policies prioritize preventive care for keeping your teeth and gums healthy. Routine cleanings, exams, and X-rays are often fully or partially covered.

Coverage may vary for other treatments, such as fillings, crowns, or emergency dental care. It’s always a good idea to check your plan details or contact our Emergency Dental of Omaha team. We can work with you to verify your benefits and determine how to maximize them for your needs.

Even if you’re unsure about your coverage, don’t let that stop you from seeking care. Our knowledgeable staff is here to guide you every step of the way.
