## **5 Common Dental Emergencies and How to Avoid Them**

Dental emergencies can strike without warning, often causing intense discomfort and disrupting your daily routine. Whether it’s sudden tooth pain, a broken tooth, or an injury to the gums, it’s essential to act quickly and seek emergency dental care. But wouldn’t it be better to avoid such emergencies altogether? At Emergency Dental of Omaha, we see many dental issues that could have been prevented with proactive care. In this blog, we’ll explore 5 common dental emergencies and share tips on avoiding them.

### 5 Common Dental Emergencies

Dental emergencies can range from minor irritations to severe conditions that need immediate attention. While you can’t prevent every dental issue, taking care of your teeth and being aware of risk factors can significantly reduce your chances of needing emergency care. Here are five common dental emergencies we see in Omaha, along with advice on how to avoid them.

#### 1. **Toothaches**

Toothaches are among the most common dental emergencies. They can range from a dull ache to sharp, intense pain. They may be caused by cavities, infections, or gum disease and often worsen without treatment.

**How to Avoid It:**

* Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste and floss regularly to keep your teeth and gums healthy.
* Schedule regular dental cleanings and exams at Emergency Dental of Omaha to catch cavities or infections before they escalate.
* Reduce your intake of sugary foods and drinks, which can lead to tooth decay.

#### 2. **Knocked-Out Tooth**

A knocked-out tooth is a severe dental emergency often caused by trauma, such as a sports injury or accident. However, if you act fast, a knocked-out tooth can frequently be saved.

**How to Avoid It:**

* If you participate in contact sports like football or hockey, always wear a mouthguard to protect your teeth.
* Avoid chewing on hard objects like ice, pens, or your fingernails, which can cause accidents or trauma to your teeth.

#### 3. **Chipped or Cracked Teeth**

Biting into hard foods or suffering an accident can lead to chipped or cracked teeth. While minor chips might not seem urgent, they can worsen and lead to more significant issues like infection or tooth loss if not treated promptly.

**How to Avoid It:**

* Be cautious when eating hard foods such as popcorn kernels or nuts.
* If you grind your teeth, especially at night, consider wearing a nightguard to prevent damage.
* Never use your teeth to open packages, leading to chips or fractures.

#### 4. [**Abscessed Tooth**](https://emergencydentalofomaha.com/service/emergency-dental-services/abscessed-tooth/)

An abscessed tooth is a severe dental infection around the root or gum that leads to pain, swelling, and even fever. If the infection spreads, it can be a life-threatening emergency, so it requires immediate attention.

**How to Avoid It:**

* Regular dental check-ups at Emergency Dental of Omaha can help prevent infections by addressing cavities or gum disease early.
* Brush and floss daily to maintain good oral hygiene and reduce the risk of bacteria buildup.

#### 5. **[Lost Fillings](https://emergencydentalofomaha.com/service/emergency-dental-services/fillings/) or [Crowns](https://emergencydentalofomaha.com/service/emergency-dental-services/crowns/)**

Lost fillings or crowns can expose the delicate interior of your tooth to bacteria, causing pain and potentially leading to infection. This situation often results from wear and tear or biting down on something hard.

**How to Avoid It:**

* Visit your dentist regularly to ensure your fillings and crowns are in good condition.
* Be careful with sticky or hard foods, such as caramel or hard candies, which can dislodge fillings or crowns.

### FAQs About 5 Common Dental Emergencies

**1. What should I do if I have a dental emergency?**
If you experience any dental emergency, [please contact Emergency Dental of Omaha](https://emergencydentalofomaha.com/contact/) right away. We offer same-day emergency services to treat urgent dental problems promptly. You can manage pain with over-the-counter medications and apply a cold compress for swelling until you arrive at our office.

**2. How do I store a knocked-out tooth?**
If your tooth is knocked out, try to place it back in the socket without touching the root. If that’s not possible, keep the tooth in a container of milk or saline solution and get to our Omaha office as soon as possible. Teeth have the best chance of being saved within one hour of being knocked out.

**3. Can I prevent dental emergencies entirely?**
While preventing every dental emergency is impossible, maintaining good oral hygiene and regularly visiting Emergency Dental of Omaha can significantly reduce your risk. Additionally, using protective gear like mouthguards can help prevent injuries during physical activity.

**4. What should I do if I lose a filling or crown?**
If you lose a filling or crown, avoid chewing on the affected side and call us immediately. You can use dental cement from a drugstore to cover the area temporarily until your appointment.

### Conclusion: 5 Common Dental Emergencies

Dental emergencies can happen to anyone, but with the proper preventive care and quick action, you can reduce the likelihood of an emergency situation. By practicing good oral hygiene, using protective mouthguards during sports, and visiting Emergency Dental of Omaha regularly, you’ll be taking the necessary steps to keep your smile healthy and avoid dental disasters.

If you do find yourself in a dental emergency, [don’t hesitate to reach out to us.](https://emergencydentalofomaha.com/contact/) Emergency Dental of Omaha is here to provide immediate, expert care when you need it most.
