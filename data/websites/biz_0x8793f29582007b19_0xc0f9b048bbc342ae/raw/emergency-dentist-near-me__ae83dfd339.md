## Frequently Asked Questions: Emergency Dentist Near Me

**Q: What should I do if I have a dental emergency?** A: If you experience a dental emergency, immediately [contact Emergency Dental of Omaha.](https://emergencydentalofomaha.com/contact/) We offer same-day appointments and extended hours to address urgent dental issues. In the meantime, please don’t touch or handle the affected area, and use cold compresses to reduce swelling.

**Q: How can I manage severe tooth pain before my appointment?** A: Rinse your mouth with warm water, floss gently to remove any trapped food, and take over-the-counter pain relievers as directed. Avoid applying aspirin directly to the affected area, which can cause irritation.

**Q: What should I do if my tooth gets knocked out?** A: If possible, retrieve the tooth by holding it by the crown, rinse it gently without scrubbing, and try to reinsert it into the socket. If this isn’t possible, place the tooth in a container of milk or saliva and bring it to our office immediately.

**Q: Can I walk in without an appointment?** A: While we accept walk-ins for emergencies, it’s always best to call ahead if possible. This allows us to prepare for your arrival and reduce your wait time.

**Q: What payment options are available for emergency services?** A: We offer various payment options, including insurance, credit cards, and flexible payment plans. Our team will work with you to ensure you receive the care you need without financial stress.
