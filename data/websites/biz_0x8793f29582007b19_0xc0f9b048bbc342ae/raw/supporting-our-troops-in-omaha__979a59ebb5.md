## Introduction: Supporting Our Troops in Omaha

From April 1st to April 19th, we at Emergency Dental of Omaha are overjoyed to share some genuinely heartwarming news—our recent partnership with Operation Care and Comfort’s Care Package Program. This initiative is special in our hearts as it supports the brave soldiers serving our country overseas.

## **What’s Happening?**

Emergency Dental has become an official collection point for donations from April 1st through the 19th in a heartfelt endeavor to give back to those who selflessly protect our freedoms. These contributions are not mere items; they are tokens of gratitude destined to become part of care packages sent to our servicemen and women abroad. It’s our gesture of giving back and building a bridge—a connection between our local community and the brave souls in uniform.

## **About Operation Care and Comfort**

At Operation Care and Comfort (OCC), the mission is clear and profoundly impactful: to honor the immense sacrifices made by our military personnel who step away from their lives to ensure the protection of United States citizens. Since its inception in 2003, OCC has remained steadfast in its commitment to bringing a touch of home to our deployed troops.

Through nationally recognized programs, OCC sends care packages overseas, provides tickets to cultural and sporting events, and offers emergency financial assistance to our veterans. These gestures of support and comfort are crucial in maintaining the connection between our soldiers and the home they valiantly defend.

## **Why It Matters: Supporting Our Troops in Omaha**

Imagine being miles away from home, in unfamiliar territories, and then receiving a package filled with the comfort of essentials and personalized mementos. That’s the feeling we aim to deliver with these care packages. They are more than just boxes; they are carriers of hope, reminders of home, and symbols that their sacrifices have not gone unnoticed. Your participation during this time sends a powerful message of support and has an immeasurable impact on our heroes’ morale and well-being.

## **How You Can Help**

There are several ways to show your support during these dates:

1. **Donate**: We welcome you to bring toiletries, non-perishable food items, sources of entertainment, or personal letters of encouragement to any Emergency Dental office.
2. **Spread the Word**: Talk about this initiative with your friends and family. Every additional person aware is another potential supporter, especially during the April 1st–19th collection period.
3. **Schedule an Appointment**: [Please schedule an appointment](https://emergencydentalofomaha.com/contact/) and drop off donation items at our Omaha office while you’re here.

## **Your Impact**

Every appointment booked and every item donated between April 1st and April 19th makes a great impact. We believe in the strength of our community and the collective efforts to show our troops they are valued and respected. It’s a testament to our unity and gratitude for those who courageously stand on the front lines for our safety.

## **Stay Informed**

To learn more about how you can help and see a detailed list of needed items, please visit Operation Care and Comfort’s official shopping list at [OCC Shopping List](https://occ-usa.org/shopping-list/) or drop by our office during the donation period. Your contributions reflect the generous spirit of our community and a deep appreciation for our troops. Let’s unite in this meaningful journey from April 1st to April 19th.

Thank you from the bottom of our hearts for your support during these crucial dates. Through these acts of kindness and unity, we can make a significant difference.
