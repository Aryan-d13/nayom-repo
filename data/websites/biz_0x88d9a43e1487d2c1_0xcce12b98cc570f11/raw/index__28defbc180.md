## THE BIOMIMETIC DIFFERENCE

Biomimetic dentistry is a form of dental care science-based that aims to mimic the natural composition of the teeth, using materials that are as close to the tooth structure as possible.

​

Biomimetic dentistry focuses on less invasive treatment methods, conserving as much natural tooth structure as possible, it relies on techniques that only removes the decayed and damaged area of your teeth, and then using advanced adhesive techniques creates a restoration that behaves naturally and is longer-lasting.

​

Biomimetic dentistry looks to preserve the dental pulp and minimize the need for root canals, remove the disease,  repair or eliminate cracks, preserve and reinforce tooth structure and delay retreatment.
