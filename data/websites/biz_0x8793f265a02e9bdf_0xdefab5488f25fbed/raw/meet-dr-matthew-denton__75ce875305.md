## What do you really enjoy?

I really enjoy spending quality time with my family—whether it’s a quiet evening at home or a fun outing. I’m also a huge sports fan, and I especially love watching Tottenham Hotspur play. When I’m not cheering on my favorite team, I enjoy being outdoors, whether it’s hiking, biking, or just enjoying nature.

## What are you most passionate about professionally?

I’d say my passion in dentistry lies in educating people about their oral health, and helping them achieve their dental goals. I really enjoy being able to offer my expertise and clinical treatment that directly benefits patients.

## What are you most passionate about personally?

[Image: denton family][Image: denton family]Spending time with family and friends. My wife and I have a two year old daughter and a Bernedoodle. My favorite weekends are when we have someone visiting or we’re able to spend the weekend traveling to a close family member or friend.

## Favorite breakfast food?

Bagels! You can’t go wrong with an everything bagel with veggie cream cheese. I worked at the Bagel Bin for two years in high school with two of my closest friends. To this day, we’ll meet up just to get the “Bin” together.

## What guided your interest in dentistry?

Growing up, I always wanted to be a Physician, like my dad. In college, I started looking more seriously into all the different job opportunities in a healthcare setting. The more I learned about the dental field, the more I was interested in becoming a dentist. After my first day shadowing a dentist, I knew it was my calling. Although it’s a commonly discussed topic, what truly drew me to dentistry was its unique blend of science and art within the healthcare field, along with the opportunity to make a meaningful impact on patients’ lives.
