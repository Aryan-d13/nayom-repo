## Benefits of Professional Teeth Whitenings

* **Whiter Teeth |** The only reason why people get their teeth whitened is because they desire a brighter smile. Professional teeth whitenings often take less than an hour and the results are immediate. Professional teeth whitening services are also much more effective than at-home, do-it-yourself teeth whitening kits. When you enter our office for a teeth whitening appointment, we will help restore your smile to its former glory and you will leave with a whiter, brighter smile.

* **No Side Effects |** At-home teeth whiteners can have harmful side effects on your teeth. Many of these store-bought kits remove the enamel that is crucial to protecting your teeth. Additionally, if these kits are used more often than recommended, your teeth can become transparent, rather than white. Professional teeth whitening services are much safer because they are performed by a licensed dentist.

* **Preventative Care |** When you come to our Omaha office for a professional teeth whitening service, your dentist will check your teeth before the whitener is applied. If there are any cavities or other issues with your teeth, the dentist will be able to take note of them and make an appointment for you to get the problem corrected.

* **Affordable |** Many people go the do-it-yourself route because they think at-home teeth whitening kits are more cost-effective than professional teeth whitenings. This, however, is not the case. Professional teeth whitening services are remarkably affordable, often costing around $100 per session. While they are more expensive than at-home teeth whitening kits, they are far more effective and are needed less often.
