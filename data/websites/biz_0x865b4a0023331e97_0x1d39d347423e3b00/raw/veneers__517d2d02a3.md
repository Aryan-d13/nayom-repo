## What are Porcelain Veneers?

Veneers are thin and natural looking. Porcelain Veneers consist of thin shells of tooth-colored porcelain that are custom made in a high quality dental laboratory. Veneers are bonded to the front of your teeth to repair and/or improve your smile. The Cosmetic Dentists of Austin can correct nearly any smile imperfection using beautiful, custom-designed porcelain veneers.

If you have crooked or misaligned teeth, porcelain veneers can be used to improve their alignment, shape and appearance to give you a beautiful new smile. Veneers are resistant to staining and discoloring, and in most cases will be stronger than your original natural teeth.

Having a smile makeover with porcelain veneers usually only takes two office visits. Your veneers will be custom designed in a style and proportion that will fit your face, look completely natural, and give you a fabulous new smile you’ll be proud to show off.

**During your second visit, your permanent dental veneers will be placed**, and you will leave our dental office with a beautiful new smile! ## Tips for Care & Maintenance for Porcelain Veneers

Proper care and maintenance of your porcelain veneers are essential to ensure they last many years and remain as beautiful as the day they were placed. Here are some guidelines to help you maintain your new smile:

1. **Regular Oral Hygiene**: Brush twice daily with a non-abrasive toothpaste and a soft-bristled toothbrush to prevent scratching the veneers. Floss daily to remove plaque and food particles between the teeth and beneath the gumline.
2. **Routine Dental Check-ups**: Visit your Austin dentist every six months for a professional cleaning and check-up. Regular visits allow your dentist to monitor the condition of your veneers and address any issues early.
3. **Avoid Hard Foods**: Although porcelain veneers are durable, biting down on hard foods like ice, nuts, or hard candy can cause them to chip or crack. It’s advisable to consume such foods cautiously.
4. **Protect Your Teeth**: If you grind or clench your teeth at night, consider wearing a night guard to protect your veneers from excessive force and potential damage.
5. **Avoid Using Teeth as Tools**: Never use your teeth to open packages, cut thread, or perform tasks that could jeopardize the integrity of your veneers.

By following these care and maintenance tips, you can enjoy your beautiful, veneer-enhanced smile for many years to come.
