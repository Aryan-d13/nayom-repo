## Invisalign Clear Aligners

[Image: cosmetic orthodontics Austin TX]

Invisalign or clear braces require the use of aligners (clear plastic trays) worn over your teeth for an extended period. Depending on the position of your teeth, it can range from 6 months to 1 year or more. The plastic trays or clear aligners must be worn for at least 22 hours daily, and sometimes additional devices, such as small attachments (commonly known as buttons), are placed on the teeth to assist treatment. Buttons are plastic or metal brackets that serve as anchor points for elastic bands (rubber bands). You cannot eat with clear braces; they must be removed at every meal. Although Invisalign or Clear Braces are presented as an easy alternative to traditional braces, they aren't as easy or seamless as you might hope. Invisalign or Clear braces may cause some pain from tooth movement.

## Traditional Metal Braces

Braces are an orthodontic treatment performed by an orthodontist to reposition/straighten your teeth. Metal braces are brackets adhered to your teeth, joined by wires and secured with small elastic bands. They cannot be removed, so there is no temptation to leave them out, so less self-discipline is needed for success than with Invisalign. In addition to their visibility (metal, typically silver), braces may cause pain, sores, or discomfort from wires, brackets, or tooth movement. Braces may also cause tooth discoloration and difficulty eating sticky, hard foods.
