#### What is Sedation Dentistry

Sedation dentistry uses safe, carefully monitored medications to help patients feel calm and comfortable during dental treatment. Whether you experience dental anxiety, have a strong gag reflex, require a lengthy procedure, or simply want a more relaxing experience, sedation can make your visit significantly more comfortable. Depending on your needs and the treatment being performed, your dentist may recommend options such as nitrous oxide (laughing gas), oral conscious sedation, or IV sedation. Throughout your appointment, your dental team will closely monitor you to ensure your safety and comfort, helping you receive the care you need with greater confidence and peace of mind.
