## What Are Dental Crowns and How Can They Improve Your Smile?

A dental crown is a durable, custom-made covering that strengthens and improves the appearance of a damaged or weakened tooth. At The Cosmetic Dentists of Austin, we specialize in porcelain crowns that seamlessly blend with your natural teeth for a beautiful, long-lasting smile.

#### Benefits of Dental Crowns

* **Restore and protect damaged teeth** – Ideal for cracked, chipped, or broken teeth
* **Improve tooth shape and structure** – Reshapes misshapen or worn-down teeth
* **Enhance your smile** – Fills embarrassing gaps or spaces between teeth
* **Whiten discolored or dull teeth** – Provides a bright, natural-looking appearance
* **Support dental restorations** – Completes [dental implants](https://www.thecosmeticdentistsofaustin.com/dental-implants/) and secures [bridges](https://www.thecosmeticdentistsofaustin.com/bridges/)

Our high-quality porcelain crowns are durable, stain-resistant, and designed to last. Many patients report a noticeable boost in confidence after restoring their teeth with natural-looking dental crowns.
