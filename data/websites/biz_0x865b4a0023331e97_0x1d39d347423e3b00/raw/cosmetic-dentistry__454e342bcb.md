## What is Cosmetic Dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of your teeth, gums, and overall smile. Unlike general dentistry, which primarily deals with preventing and treating oral diseases, cosmetic dentistry emphasizes aesthetics and function. Whether you are looking to repair a chipped tooth, brighten your smile, or achieve a full transformation, cosmetic dentistry provides tailored solutions to enhance your appearance and confidence. Cosmetic treatments often provide more than just visual improvements. Many procedures also strengthen teeth, improve oral health, and restore proper function. Whether you are looking for minor enhancements or comprehensive changes, our team is ready to guide you through the possibilities.

## Who is a Good Candidate for Cosmetic Dentistry?

If you are unhappy with the appearance of your teeth or gums, you may be an excellent candidate for cosmetic dentistry. Common issues that cosmetic treatments can address include stained or discolored teeth, chipped or cracked teeth, gaps between teeth, uneven gum lines, and missing teeth.

You may also benefit from cosmetic dentistry if you are looking to enhance your overall facial aesthetics, boost your self-esteem, or restore the function of damaged teeth. Good candidates should have healthy gums and a commitment to maintaining their oral hygiene after treatment to ensure long-lasting results.

We take time during your first appointment to assess your oral health, understand your cosmetic goals, and create a customized plan for your perfect smile.
