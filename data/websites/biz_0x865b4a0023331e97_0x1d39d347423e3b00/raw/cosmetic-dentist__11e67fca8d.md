"I’m often asked what made me want to become a dentist. It probably had a great deal to do with the fact that for more than half of my life, I was embarrassed by my own teeth. Recently, my wife and I were looking through old photographs that go all the way back to our high school days (yes, I married my high school sweetheart). We began to notice that there was not one picture of me smiling where my teeth could be seen. Sadly, this was even true of our wedding photographs. After dental school, I was finally able to have my own smile corrected with porcelain veneers. What a truly life changing event. From then on I was hooked on cosmetic dentistry. I wanted to help everyone know the feeling of freedom and confidence that comes from being able to laugh and smile without hesitation. I feel particularly fortunate to have been in the field of dentistry during a period of enormous progress. Today, porcelain veneers are so lifelike that even I have a hard time telling them from the real thing. Unlike the bulky, flat white ‘piano keys’ of old, these veneers allow light to pass through and have all the tiny, subtle characteristics that you expect to see in a beautiful natural tooth."
- John Christian Schiro DDS

## Meet John D. Barras, DDS, LVIF

### LVIF (Las Vegas Institute For Advanced Dental Studies)

[Image: barras 1.2x]

Dr. John D. Barras grew up in Orange, a small town in Southeast, Texas. From an early age he was scholastic, and on the day of his high school graduation, Dr. John D. Barras was recognized and received a standing ovation from peers for maintaining perfect attendance throughout his grade school years, K through 12th. All while maintaining his position as one of the top students in his class it was at an early age Dr. John D. Barras realized his hard work and dedication would be the catalyst for several earned scholarships paving his way into college.

Dr. John D. Barras received his undergraduate degree from Southwest Texas State with a bachelor’s degree in Biology, and a minor in Chemistry.

Soon after, he was accepted into dental school at the University of Texas Dental Branch in Houston, Texas, receiving his DDS in 2002. Post graduation, Dr. Barras served for various dental opportunities including: A mobile dental clinic serving the assisted living and medically compromised patients and dental clinics in underprivileged areas serving patients with general and cosmetic dentistry needs. It was after volunteering for a dental program in Nicaragua, he realized his enthusiasm for dentistry was unparalleled and with the love and support from family and friends, he was able to open his first private practice in the prestigious Upper Kirby District in Houston, Texas in 2004.

His growth, success and passion to serve his patients lead him to add a second practice in the prominent Post Oak District, Galleria area in Houston. The opportunity to work with a highly esteemed mentor who was ready to retire sold Dr. Barras the practice in 2009, and it was in 2011 he sold his first practice focusing on Dentistry by John Barras, DDS in order to further advance his knowledge of Dentistry with continuing education at the rigorous Las Vegas Institute for Advanced Dental Studies in Las Vegas, Nevada. There he perfected his craft in Cosmetics and Neuromuscular Dentistry and Dr. Barras earned his LVIF in 2014.

For Dr. Barras, the cosmetic aspect of serving his patients with smile makeovers is a bonus. However, it is his zeal for helping those suffering with TMJ and TMD symptoms, and studying the comprehension of each patient’s unique bite to aesthetically ensure the cosmetic aspect lasts a lifetime, while getting his patients out of pain is not only gratifying but also a privilege.
