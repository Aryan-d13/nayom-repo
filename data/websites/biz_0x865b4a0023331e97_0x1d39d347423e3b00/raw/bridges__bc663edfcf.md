## What Are Dental Bridges?

A dental bridge provides a stable, long-term replacement for missing teeth while maintaining proper oral function and appearance. The pontic, or artificial tooth, is supported by crowns placed on surrounding natural teeth or secured with [dental implants](https://www.thecosmeticdentistsofaustin.com/dental-implants/) for a seamless fit. Beyond restoring your smile, a bridge reinforces bite stability and stops adjacent teeth from shifting.

Many patients opt for porcelain dental bridges due to their natural appearance. Crafted to blend seamlessly with surrounding teeth, porcelain bridges mimic the look and feel of real enamel, providing both durability and aesthetic appeal.

Based on your oral health and tooth loss, a traditional, cantilever, or implant-supported bridge may be the best solution. Each type is designed to restore function and aesthetics in different ways. The Cosmetic Dentists of Austin can help determine which solution will best complement your smile and provide lasting results.

## Benefits of Dental Bridges

Dental bridges provide numerous advantages for those looking to restore their smile after tooth loss. Some of the key advantages include:

* Improving chewing efficiency and speech clarity
* Keeping surrounding teeth stable and properly aligned
* Preserving facial structure by supporting the jaw and surrounding tissues
* Providing a long-lasting solution when properly cared for
* Offering a non-invasive alternative to dental implants for tooth replacement

## The Dental Bridge Procedure

The process of getting a dental bridge usually takes two visits to achieve a secure and natural fit.

### First Visit:

1. Your dentist will assess your teeth, review your dental needs, and decide if a bridge is the right solution.
2. X-rays or digital impressions may be taken to assist with planning.
3. When opting for a traditional or porcelain bridge, the neighboring teeth will be prepared to hold supporting crowns.
4. Molds or digital scans of your teeth will be taken to design a bridge that fits precisely.
5. A temporary bridge may be used to safeguard your teeth while your permanent bridge is being made.

### Second Visit:

1. Your final porcelain bridge, designed as a single, natural-looking piece with crowns on the anchor teeth and a pontic tooth in between, will be ready.
2. The dentist will ensure a precise fit and make any necessary adjustments.
3. The bridge will be securely bonded to your natural teeth with dental cement for a seamless result.
4. To promote long-term success, your dentist may schedule follow-up visits as needed.
5. A well-maintained bridge can last for years with proper oral hygiene and consistent dental care.
