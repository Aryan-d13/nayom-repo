## Eat Well, Drink Deep, Stay Awhile.

At Salty Sow, we’re serving more than just great food–we’re dishing out gatherings, good times, and unforgettable meals. [Chef Harold Marmulstein](/about) blends slow-cooked Southern tradition with bold, fresh flavors that are pure Texas comfort, mindfully sourced from local farms and purveyors. Whether you’re here for a laid-back happy hour or to unwind over dinner, you’ll experience a [warm, welcoming atmosphere that feels like home.](/about)
