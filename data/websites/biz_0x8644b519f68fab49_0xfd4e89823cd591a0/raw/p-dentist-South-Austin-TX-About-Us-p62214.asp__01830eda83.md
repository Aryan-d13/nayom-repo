| About Sola Smile Co.  [Image: Reception and wait area at Sola Smile Co. in Austin, TX]At Sola Smile Co. we believe the mouth is the gateway to the body—and we're committed to treating you with a whole-body, wellness-focused approach.    Located in South Austin, we are a privately owned dental practice offering comprehensive care in a spa-like, relaxing, and judgment-free environment.   Led by Dr. Audrey Su and Dr. Carson Jett, our team of highly trained providers offers a wide range of services, including general and cosmetic dentistry, [Invisalign](/p/dentist-South-Austin-TX-Invisalign-p62282.asp "Invisalign Sola Smile Co. - Dr. Audrey Su Austin, TX dentist South Austin TX"), and [biological dental services](/p/dentist-South-Austin-TX-Biological-Dentistry-p62303.asp "Biological Dentistry Sola Smile Co. - Dr. Audrey Su Austin, TX dentist South Austin TX") such as safe mercury removal (SMART).    We utilize state-of-the-art technology to provide comfortable and convenient same-day solutions.   We also partner with a trusted network of skilled specialists to ensure every aspect of your care is thoughtfully coordinated and expertly delivered.   Our team is passionate about continued learning and professional growth so we can provide the highest standard of care for our patients. Founded in 2011 by Dr. Thomas Gent, Sola Smile Co. continues to uphold a legacy of excellence as one of Austin’s premier dental practices.   We’re truly honored to be part of your health journey—and we can’t wait to welcome you!
