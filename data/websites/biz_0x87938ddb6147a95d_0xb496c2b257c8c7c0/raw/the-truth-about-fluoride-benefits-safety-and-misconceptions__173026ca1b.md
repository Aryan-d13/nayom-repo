## What Fluoride Does for Your Teeth

Fluoride is a naturally occurring mineral that helps protect teeth from decay. It strengthens tooth enamel, making it more resistant to acid produced by bacteria in the mouth. Regular exposure to fluoride, whether through drinking water or professional treatments, has been proven to reduce the risk of cavities significantly.

In dental offices, fluoride treatment is often applied as a gel, foam, or varnish. Those at higher risk for cavities or enamel thinning may find fluoride treatment especially effective.

## The Real Benefits of Fluoride Treatment

Fluoride treatments offer both short-term and long-term protection. These benefits include:

* Strengthening tooth enamel to resist decay
* Slowing or even reversing early signs of tooth decay
* Providing extra protection for patients with dry mouth or gum disease
* Supporting children’s developing teeth and maintaining adult oral health

For many Omaha families, fluoride treatments are a simple and affordable way to maintain healthy smiles between dental visits.

## Common Fluoride Myths

Despite its widespread use and scientific support, several myths about fluoride persist. Let’s set the record straight:

* **Myth**: Fluoride is dangerous or toxic.
* **Fact:** When used in recommended amounts, fluoride is completely safe. The American Dental Association (ADA) and Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) endorse its use.
* **Myth:** Fluoride is unnecessary with proper brushing.
* **Fact:** Brushing helps, but fluoride provides a protective layer that brushing alone cannot match.
* **Myth:** Children should avoid fluoride.
* **Fact:** In fact, fluoride is crucial during childhood when teeth are still developing.

## Is Fluoride Safe? Yes, Absolutely.

Fluoride has been studied extensively for decades. Health and dental organizations agree that fluoride is safe and effective when used appropriately. The fluoride used in dental treatments is applied in controlled amounts by professionals. In Omaha, the community water supply also contains safe fluoride levels to support oral health across the population.
