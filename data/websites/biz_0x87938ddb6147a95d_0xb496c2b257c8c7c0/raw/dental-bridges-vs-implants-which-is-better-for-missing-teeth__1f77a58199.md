## What Is a Dental Bridge?

A dental bridge is an artificial tooth that fills the gap left by a missing one, anchored by crowns on the surrounding natural teeth. It’s a non-surgical solution that bridges the gap a missing tooth leaves.

Some advantages of dental bridges include:

* Typically faster to complete than implants
* Often less expensive upfront
* Effective at restoring function and appearance

However, bridges may require modification of healthy teeth and usually need to be replaced every 7 to 10 years.

## What Is a Dental Implant?

Dental implants are titanium roots that are surgically placed in the jawbone and topped with a crown for a natural-looking result.

Key benefits of dental implants:

* Long-lasting (often 15 years or more with proper care)
* Help preserve jawbone health
* Look and function like natural teeth
* Do not rely on neighboring teeth for support

The implant process takes longer and involves surgery but offers a more permanent and independent solution.

## Comparing Dental Bridges and Implants

Several factors come into play when choosing between a dental bridge and an implant. Here’s a quick comparison:

* **Cost**: Bridges typically cost less upfront; implants may save money long-term.
* **Longevity**: Implants often last longer with proper care.
* **Time**: Bridges are faster to complete; implants require healing time.
* **Procedure**: Bridges are non-surgical; implants involve minor oral surgery.
* **Oral Health Impact**: Implants support jawbone preservation; bridges require support from neighboring teeth.

## Which Option Is Right for You?

There is no one-size-fits-all answer. The best treatment depends on your oral health, budget, bone structure, and personal goals. Some patients may not be candidates for implants due to insufficient bone or other medical conditions, while others may prefer the long-term benefits implants offer.
