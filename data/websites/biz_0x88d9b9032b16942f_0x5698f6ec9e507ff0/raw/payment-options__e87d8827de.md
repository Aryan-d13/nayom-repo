# Patient Information

We know good dental care is not always within reach for everyone. At South Florida Dentistry, we always go above and beyond to ensure patients receive the care they need. South Florida Dentistry works with almost all PPO insurances, Medicare, and offers multiple payment options listed below.

### Cherry

Our patients' #1 choice for payment. Think of this as a payment plan vs a credit card. It allows you to pay for your treatment upfront so you can get the care you need without the wait, all while offering low monthly payments.

### In House Plan

We created this plan when we saw a gap for patients who do not have dental insurance and may not qualify for other credit based options. This gives you year round coverage and offers discount on treatment.

**No insurance? No Problem!**
South Florida Dentistry offers an In-Office Plan to give you the care you need year-round!

**How does it work?**
Enroll for $99 on the day of your initial visit. This will include your X-rays, exam, and cleaning.

We will then let you know what plan fits best for you based on the type of cleaning you need.

All of our plans include:

* 2-3 cleanings per year
* Comprehensive and periodontal exams
* 20% discount on necessary treatment
* Panoramic or full series x-rays
