Q: How often should I visit the dentist for a check-up?
A: <p>We recommend coming to visit us 2-4 times a year. This is specific to each individual, and we will always let you know what schedule is best for you.</p>

Q:  Is fluoride good or bad for you?
A: <p>Fluoride in moderation is good for you. It helps strengthen your enamel, reduce sensitivity, and can even reverse small cavities. In Miami-Dade County, fluoride has been removed from the water, so it is even more important now to receive your fluoride treatment twice a year during your cleaning visits.</p>

Q: What are the benefits of dental implants compared to other tooth replacement options?
A: <p>The benefits of dental implants are that they are the closest thing to mimicking your full natural tooth structure. In comparison to other tooth replacements, they do not affect the neighboring teeth, such as bridges do.</p>

Q:   How can I whiten my teeth safely at home or with professional treatments?
A: <p>Whitening at home should be customized and guided by a professional for maximum results. We offer in-office whitening for the fastest results, but are happy to recommend at-home products based on what is best for the individual.</p>

Q: What are the causes of bad breath, and how can it be treated?
A: <p>Bad breath can be caused by several different things. Gingivitis, cavities, even your own PH can all play a role in causing bad odor. Ask your provider about what you are experiencing and what treatment can help you.</p>

Q: How long should I wait between dental visits?
A: <p>Typically 6 months if there is no ongoing treatment or gum issues.</p>

Q:  What is the difference between a dentist and an orthodontist?
A: <p>An orthodontist is a specialist; this means that the individual went to school for additional time to specialize in this field. </p>

Q: Are there natural remedies for toothaches or gum pain that actually work?
A: <p>If you are experiencing a toothache or gum pain, it is important that you make an appointment to find out why you are having pain. Do not try to resolve it on your own, as home remedies can potentially cause more harm.</p>

Q: Do root canals hurt?
A: <p>Root canals are performed with local anesthetics; there should be no pain during the procedure.</p>

Q: What are my options for pain relief during dental treatments?
A: <p>During dental treatments, we can alleviate pain with topical or local anesthetics.</p>

Q: Do you do sedation dentistry?
A: <p>We do not do sedation dentistry. If you feel it is necessary, we can always refer you to another practice.</p>

Q: I brush and floss my teeth every day, but why do I still get cavities?
A: <p>Cavities can be caused by many different reasons and can occur even if you have good hygiene practices. Lifestyle, diet, and genetics play a large role in why cavities occur.</p>

Q: What happens during my first dental visit?
A: <p>During your first visit to South Florida Dentistry, we will take your X-rays, perform a cleaning, and do a check-up exam with the Doctor. If you are coming in with a specific issue, we will begin with X-rays and an exam with the Doctor to determine what is happening.</p>

Q: Is dental care safe for children?
A: <p>Dental care is safe and necessary for children. Baby teeth get cavities the same as adult teeth, which can lead to pain and infection if not cared for.</p>

Q: At what age should I take my child in for their first dental exam?
A: <p>Children should go in for their first dental exam by their first birthday, but can also go as early as 6 months.</p>
