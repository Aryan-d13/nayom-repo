## What are Clear Aligners?

Clear aligners are transparent, removable dental appliances that gradually shift your teeth into their desired positions. Unlike traditional metal braces, these aligners are virtually invisible, making them an attractive option for adults and teens. Each set of aligners is custom-made to fit your mouth perfectly, ensuring maximum comfort and effectiveness throughout your treatment journey.

## How Do Clear Aligners Work?

The process of getting clear aligners at Florida Dental Studio involves several key steps:

Professional treatments use higher concentrations of whitening agents, such as hydrogen peroxide or carbamide peroxide, for more effective and longer-lasting results.

Using these scans, our team will develop a detailed treatment plan that maps out the exact movements of your teeth over time. You will be able to see a digital preview of your future smile and understand the stages of your treatment.

You receive a series of custom aligners that you wear sequentially. Each set of aligners is designed to be worn for about two weeks, gradually moving your teeth closer to their final positions.

Aligners should be worn for 20-22 hours daily, only removed for meals, drinking (other than water), brushing, and flossing. Regular check-ups with Dr. Gavilla or Dr. Gonzalez will ensure your treatment progresses as planned.

Once your treatment is complete, we may provide a retainer to maintain your new smile and prevent any shifting.

##### 1

##### Discreet and Comfortable

Clear aligners are virtually invisible, allowing you to straighten your teeth without drawing unwanted attention to your treatment. They’re also smooth and comfortable, eliminating the irritation often associated with traditional braces.

##### 2

##### Removable for Eating and Cleaning

Unlike traditional braces, the clear aligners can easily be removed for eating, brushing, and flossing. This makes maintaining proper oral hygiene more convenient and allows you to enjoy your favorite foods without restrictions.

##### 3

##### Customized Treatment Plan

Each set of aligners is custom-made to fit your unique dental structure, ensuring a precise and personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs.

##### 4

##### Improved Oral Health

Straighter teeth are easier to clean, reducing the risk of tooth decay, gum disease, and other dental problems arising from crowded or misaligned teeth.
