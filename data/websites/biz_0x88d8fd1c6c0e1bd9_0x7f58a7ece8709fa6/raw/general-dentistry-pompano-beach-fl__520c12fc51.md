## Our General Dentistry Services

At Florida Dental Studio, we offer comprehensive general dentistry services to keep your smile healthy and beautiful. Our services include:

##### Preventive Dentistry

Preventive dentistry focuses on maintaining oral health and preventing dental problems before they occur. Our preventive services include:

* **Routine Check-ups**: Regular dental exams are essential for detecting and addressing dental issues early. During your check-up, Dr. Gavilla or Dr. Gonzalez will thoroughly examine your teeth, gums, and mouth to identify any problems.
* **Professional Cleanings**: Professional dental cleanings remove plaque and tartar buildup that regular brushing and flossing can’t reach. Our hygienists will also polish your teeth, leaving them clean and smooth.
* **X-rays**: Dental X-rays provide a detailed view of your teeth and jawbone, helping us detect issues such as cavities, bone loss, and impacted teeth that are not visible during a regular exam.
* **Fluoride Treatments**: Fluoride treatments strengthen your teeth and help prevent cavities, particularly for children and those at higher risk of tooth decay.

**Sealants**: Dental sealants are thin coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of molars to protect them from cavities. They are especially beneficial for children and teenagers.

##### Restorative Dentistry

Restorative dentistry aims to repair and restore the function and appearance of damaged or missing teeth. Our restorative services include:

* **Fillings**: We use tooth-colored composite fillings to repair cavities and restore the integrity of your teeth.
* **Crowns**: Dental crowns are custom-made caps that cover damaged or weakened teeth, restoring their strength and appearance.
* **Bridges**: Dental bridges replace one or more missing teeth, using adjacent teeth as anchors. They restore your smile and improve your ability to chew and speak.
* **Dentures**: We offer partial and complete dentures to replace missing teeth and restore your smile.
* **Implants**: Dental implants are a permanent solution for missing teeth, consisting of a titanium post that fuses with your jawbone and a custom crown that looks and functions like a natural tooth.
* **Root Canals**: Root canal therapy treats infected or damaged tooth pulp, saving the tooth and alleviating pain.

##### Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry focuses on improving the appearance of your teeth and smile. Our cosmetic services include:

* **Teeth Whitening**: Professional teeth whitening treatments can significantly brighten your smile by removing stains and discoloration.
* **Veneers**: Veneers are thin, custom-made shells that cover the front surface of your teeth to correct imperfections such as chips, gaps, and discoloration.
* **Bonding**: Dental bonding uses tooth-colored resin to repair minor imperfections like chips, cracks, and gaps, enhancing the appearance of your teeth.

**Invisalign**: Clear aligners provide a discreet and comfortable way to straighten your teeth and correct misalignments.

##### Emergency Dentistry

Dental emergencies can happen at any time, causing pain and anxiety. At Florida Dental Studio, we offer prompt and effective emergency dental care to address urgent issues such as:

* **Toothaches**: Severe tooth pain can indicate an underlying issue that requires immediate attention.
* **Chipped or Broken Teeth**: Accidents or injuries can cause teeth to chip or break, requiring urgent repair.
* **Knocked-Out Teeth**: Quick action is crucial to save a knocked-out tooth and reinsert it successfully.

**Lost Fillings or Crowns**: If a filling or crown comes loose, you must replace it promptly to protect the tooth.
