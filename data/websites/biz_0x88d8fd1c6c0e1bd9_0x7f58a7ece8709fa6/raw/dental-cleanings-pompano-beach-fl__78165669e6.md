## What is a Dental Cleaning?

A dental cleaning, also known as prophylaxis, is a professional cleaning procedure performed by a dentist or dental hygienist to remove plaque, tartar, and stains from your teeth. Even with diligent at-home oral care, plaque and tartar can build up in hard-to-reach areas, potentially leading to gum disease and tooth decay. Dental cleanings are crucial in preventing these issues and promoting overall oral health.

## How Often Should You Get Your Teeth Cleaned?

The American Dental Association (ADA) recommends that most individuals have their teeth professionally cleaned at least twice a year. However, some people may require more frequent cleanings based on their specific oral health needs. Factors such as a history of gum disease, smoking, or a tendency to develop plaque quickly can necessitate more regular visits. During your consultation at Florida Dental Studio, Dr. Gavilla or Dr. Gonzalez will assess your oral health and recommend a personalized cleaning schedule.

## What are the Benefits of a Professional Cleaning?

Professional dental cleanings offer numerous benefits beyond what regular brushing and flossing can achieve:

* **Prevents Gum Disease:** Regular cleanings help remove plaque and tartar that can lead to gingivitis and periodontitis, ensuring healthier gums.
* **Prevents Tooth Decay:** Cleanings remove plaque, which is a significant cause of tooth decay, reducing the risk of cavities.
* **Freshens Breath:** Removing plaque and tartar reduces bacteria in the mouth, which can help eliminate bad breath.
* **Brightens Your Smile:** Cleanings remove surface stains caused by food, drinks, and tobacco, resulting in a brighter, whiter smile.
* **Detects Oral Health Issues Early:** Regular visits allow your dentist to detect and address potential problems, such as cavities, gum disease, or oral cancer.
* **Overall Health Benefits:** Good oral health is linked to overall health, with studies showing that regular cleanings can reduce the risk of heart disease, stroke, and other systemic conditions.

## What to Expect During Your Dental Cleaning

At Florida Dental Studio, your comfort and care are our top priorities. Here’s what you can expect during your dental cleaning appointment:

1. **Oral Examination**: Before the cleaning begins, Dr. Gavilla or Dr. Gonzalez will perform a thorough examination of your mouth to check for any signs of oral health issues such as cavities, gum disease, or other concerns.
2. **Removing Plaque and Tartar**: Using specialized tools, your dental hygienist will remove plaque and tartar from the surfaces of your teeth and along the gumline. This process, known as scaling, targets the buildup that regular brushing and flossing can’t eliminate.
3. **Deep Cleaning**: After scaling, the hygienist will perform root planing if necessary. This deep cleaning involves cleaning the roots of your teeth to remove bacteria and smooth the tooth root to prevent future buildup.
4. **Polishing**: Once the teeth are free of plaque and tartar, your hygienist will polish them using gritty toothpaste and a high-powered brush. This step removes surface stains and leaves your teeth smooth and shiny.
5. **Fluoride Treatment**: To help strengthen your teeth and protect them from cavities, we may apply a fluoride treatment. Fluoride can come in the form of gel, foam, or varnish.

**Final Check and Recommendations**: Dr. Gavilla or Dr. Gonzalez will conduct a final check of your teeth and gums, provide any necessary recommendations for further treatment, and answer any questions about your oral health.

#### Brushing Tips

* **Use the Right Brush**: Choose a soft-bristled toothbrush that fits comfortably in your mouth. Electric toothbrushes can also be very effective.
* **Brush Twice Daily**: Brush your teeth at least twice daily, ideally after meals.
* **Proper Technique**: Hold your toothbrush at a 45-degree angle to your gums and use gentle, circular motions. Ensure you brush all surfaces of your teeth, including the outer, inner, and chewing surfaces.
* **Brush for Two Minutes**: Spend at least two minutes brushing to ensure a thorough clean.
* **Don’t Forget Your Tongue**: Brush your tongue to remove bacteria and freshen your breath.

#### Flossing Tips

* **Use Enough Floss**: Break off about 18 inches of floss and wind it around your middle fingers, leaving a couple of inches to work with.
* **Be Gentle**: Gently slide the floss between your teeth using a back-and-forth motion. Avoid snapping it into your gums.
* **Curved Flossing**: Curve the floss around each tooth in a C-shape and gently slide it under the gumline and along the side of the tooth.
* **Use Clean Sections**: Use a clean section of floss for each tooth to avoid transferring plaque and bacteria.
