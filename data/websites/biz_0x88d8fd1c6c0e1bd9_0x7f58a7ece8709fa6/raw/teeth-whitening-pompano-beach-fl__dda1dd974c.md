## What is Professional Teeth Whitening?

Professional teeth whitening is a dental procedure performed by a qualified dentist or dental professional to remove stains and discoloration from the tooth enamel, revealing a brighter, whiter smile. Unlike over-the-counter whitening products, professional treatments use higher concentrations of whitening agents and are administered under the supervision of a dental professional, ensuring optimal results and safety.

## Professional vs. Over-the-Counter Whitening

While over-the-counter whitening products may provide some level of whitening, they typically use lower concentrations of whitening agents and are designed for short-term use. Professional teeth whitening treatments, on the other hand, offer several advantages:

Professional treatments use higher concentrations of whitening agents, such as hydrogen peroxide or carbamide peroxide, for more effective and longer-lasting results.

Dr. Gavilla or Dr. Gonzalez can customize the whitening process based on your specific needs and desired level of whiteness, ensuring optimal results and minimizing sensitivity.

#### Avoid Staining Foods and Beverages:

Limit your consumption of products that can stain your teeth, such as coffee, tea, red wine, and deeply colored foods.

#### Quit Smoking:

Smoking is a significant contributor to tooth discoloration and can quickly undo the results of your whitening treatment.

#### Practice Good Oral Hygiene:

Brush and floss regularly to prevent the buildup of plaque and stains on your teeth.

#### Use Whitening Toothpaste:

Incorporate a whitening toothpaste into your daily routine to help maintain your bright smile.

#### Schedule Touch-Up Treatments:

Dr. Gavilla or Dr. Gonzalez may recommend touch-up whitening treatments every six to twelve months for optimal results.
