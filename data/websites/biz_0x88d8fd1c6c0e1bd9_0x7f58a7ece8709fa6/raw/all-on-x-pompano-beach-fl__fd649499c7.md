## What Are All-on-4s?

All-on-4 is an advanced dental implant technique designed to replace an entire arch of missing teeth using just four strategically placed implants. This treatment concept offers a stable, permanent solution for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth in one or both jaws. The implants are typically made of titanium and are surgically placed into the jawbone. These implants then support an entire arch of prosthetic teeth, providing a natural-looking and fully functional smile.

## Why Should You Replace Your Missing Teeth?

Replacing missing teeth is crucial for several reasons:

* **Oral Health:** Missing teeth can lead to bone loss in the jaw, which can cause the remaining teeth to shift and lead to further oral health issues.
* **Functionality:** Teeth play a vital role in chewing and speaking. Missing teeth can affect your ability to eat a balanced diet and articulate words clearly.
* **Aesthetics:** Missing teeth can make you feel self-conscious and affect your confidence. Restoring your smile can improve your overall appearance.
* **Overall Health**: Poor oral health due to missing teeth can contribute to systemic health problems, including heart disease and diabetes.

## What are the Benefits of All-on-4s?

All-on-4 dental implants offer numerous benefits, making them an ideal solution for full-arch replacement:

##### 1

##### Stability and Comfort

Unlike traditional dentures, All-on-4 implants are anchored securely in the jawbone, providing a stable and comfortable fit.

##### 2

##### Immediate Results

In many cases, a temporary set of teeth can be placed on the same day as the implants, allowing you to leave the office with a functional smile.

##### 3

##### Preserve Jawbone

The implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone loss that typically occurs with missing teeth and maintaining your facial structure.

##### 4

##### Cost-Effective

All-on-4s often require fewer implants and less invasive procedures than traditional full-mouth implant techniques, making them more affordable.

##### 5

##### Easy Maintenance

Caring for All-on-4 implants is similar to caring for natural teeth, involving regular brushing, flossing, and dental check-ups.

##### 6

##### Long-Lasting

With proper care, All-on-4 implants can last a lifetime, providing a durable and permanent solution for tooth loss.

#### Temporary Prosthesis

In many cases, a temporary set of teeth is attached to the implants on the same day, allowing you to leave the office with a functional and aesthetically pleasing smile.

#### Healing Period

Over the next few months, the implants will fuse with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration. It is important to follow post-operative care instructions to ensure proper healing during this time.

#### Final Prosthesis

Once the implants have fully integrated with the bone, you will return to our office to receive your final, custom-made prosthetic teeth. These teeth look and feel like natural teeth, providing a perfect fit and a beautiful smile.

#### Initial Consultation

Your journey begins with a thorough consultation with Dr. Gavilla or Dr. Gonzalez. During this visit, we will discuss your dental history, perform a comprehensive examination, and take necessary imaging (such as X-rays and CT scans) to assess your oral health and bone structure.

#### Treatment Planning

We will create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs and goals based on the information gathered. This plan includes the number and placement of implants, the type of prosthetic teeth, and the timeline for the procedure.

#### Surgical Procedure

On the day of the surgery, we will precisely place the dental implants into the jawbone. This step is typically done under local anesthesia, but sedation options are available for your comfort. The implants are positioned at strategic angles to maximize stability and support.
