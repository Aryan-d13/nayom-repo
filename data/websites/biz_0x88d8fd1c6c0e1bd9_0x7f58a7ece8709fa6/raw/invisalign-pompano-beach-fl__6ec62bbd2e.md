## What is Invisalign?

Invisalign is an advanced orthodontic system that gradually straightens teeth using a series of clear, removable aligners. Unlike traditional braces, Invisalign aligners are virtually invisible, allowing you to improve your smile without the noticeable appearance of metal brackets and wires. Each aligner is custom-made to fit snugly over your teeth and gently shift them into the desired position over time.

## What are the Benefits of Invisalign?

Choosing Invisalign offers numerous advantages over conventional braces, making it popular for teens and adults. Here are some of the key benefits:

The clear aligners are nearly invisible, making them an excellent option for those who want a subtle solution.

Made from smooth, BPA-free plastic, Invisalign aligners are comfortable and less likely to irritate your gums and cheeks than metal braces.

You can easily remove the aligners for eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing, allowing you to maintain optimal oral hygiene and enjoy your favorite foods without restriction.

Invisalign requires fewer office visits than traditional braces since you receive multiple sets of aligners at once and only need check-ups approximately every 6-8 weeks.

The treatment planning software lets you see a virtual representation of your teeth’s movement, previewing your expected results.

## What Can Invisalign Help Correct?

Invisalign is a versatile treatment that can address a wide range of orthodontic issues, including:

##### 1

##### Crowded Teeth

When there isn’t enough space in your jaw for all your teeth to fit normally.

##### 2

##### Gapped Teeth

When there is too much space between teeth, often due to abnormal jaw growth or missing teeth.

##### 3

##### Overbite

When the upper front teeth overlap significantly with the lower teeth.

##### 4

##### Underbite

When the lower teeth protrude past the upper front teeth.

##### 4

##### Crossbite

When some upper teeth sit inside the lower teeth rather than outside.

##### 4

##### Open Bite

When the upper and lower teeth don’t touch when the mouth is closed.

## What to Expect When Getting Invisalign and How Does It Work?

At Florida Dental Studio, your journey to a perfect smile with Invisalign involves several key steps:

1. **Initial Consultation:** During your first visit, Dr. Gavilla or Dr. Gonzalez will comprehensively examine your teeth and discuss your orthodontic goals. We will take digital impressions and photographs of your teeth to create a precise 3D model.
2. **Custom Treatment Plan:** Using advanced Invisalign software, we will map out a detailed treatment plan, including the exact movements of your teeth and the duration of the treatment. You’ll even get a sneak peek at your new smile
3. **Receive Your Aligners:** You’ll receive several sets to start your treatment once your custom aligners are ready. Each set of aligners is worn for about 1-2 weeks, depending on your specific plan, and should be worn for 20-22 hours daily.
4. **Progress Check-ups:** You will have regular check-ups approximately every 6-8 weeks with Dr. Gavilla or Dr. Gonzalez to ensure your treatment is progressing as planned and to receive your next set of aligners.
5. **Completion and Retainers:** After completing your Invisalign treatment, you may need to wear retainers to help maintain your new smile. Retainers are custom-made and typically worn at night.

#### Clean Your Aligners DailyClean Your Aligners Daily

Rinse your aligners every time you remove them. Brush them gently with a soft toothbrush and clear, antibacterial soap or Invisalign cleaning crystals. Avoid using toothpaste, as it can be abrasive and cause scratches.

#### Soak Your Aligners

Use Invisalign cleaning crystals or a denture cleaner to soak your aligners daily. This cleaner helps remove plaque and keep it fresh.

#### Handle with Care

Always remove your aligners carefully using your fingertips, and avoid using sharp objects that could damage them.

#### Store Properly

When not wearing your aligners, keep them in their protective case to prevent loss or damage.

#### Avoid Staining

To keep your aligners clear, avoid eating or drinking anything other than water while wearing them. Colored beverages and foods can stain the aligners.
