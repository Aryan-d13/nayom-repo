# About Us

## Dr. Lazaro Gavilla

DDS

Dr. Lazaro F. Gavilla earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the Universidad Central del Este in the Dominican Republic. He then completed a two-year residency at Columbia University College of Dental Medicine and spent the next year as Chief Resident for the Advance Education in General Dentistry Program at New York Presbyterian Hospital. Dr. Gavilla received training in Comprehensive Treatment Planning, including full-mouth rehabilitation and Serial Extraction Protocol for implant patients, cosmetic dentistry, and complex cases.

He has remained current on the latest developments in his field, including those in cosmetics, digital, and implant dentistry. He also remains active in the dental community as a member of the American Dental Association, American Academy of Implant Prosthodontics, International Congress of Oral Implantology, and Hispanic Dental Association.

In his free time, Dr. Gavilla enjoys listening to music, boating, playing basketball, and spending time with his family.

## Dr. Sutnamis Gonzalez

DDS

Dr. Sutnamis Gonzalez is a highly skilled dentist who has been serving the South Florida community for many years. Dr. Gonzalez is no stranger to dentistry; in fact, she earned her dental degree from Universidad Central del Este – San Pedro de Macoris and completed two years of advanced training in General Dentistry at Columbia University.

Dr. Gonzalez is passionate about providing the highest quality dental care to her patients and offers various procedures such as preventative care, restorative treatments, and cosmetic procedures while keeping up with the latest techniques and advancements in dental technology.

Aside from being clinically proficient, Dr. Gonzalez also possesses a warm bedside manner that makes her patients feel relaxed and at ease. Her genuine concern for their well-being is evident as she takes the time to listen attentively to their concerns while crafting personalized treatment plans that are tailored specifically according to their needs—ensuring all patients receive optimal care, every time they visit.

Outside of her practice, Dr. Gonzalez is actively involved in local dental organizations and is committed to giving back to her community through volunteer work.
