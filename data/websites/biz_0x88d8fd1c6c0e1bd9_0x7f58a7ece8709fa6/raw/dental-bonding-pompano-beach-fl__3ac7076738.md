## What is Dental Bonding?

Dental bonding is a cosmetic dental procedure that involves applying a tooth-colored resin material to the teeth and hardening it with a special light. This bonds the material to the tooth, improving its appearance. It’s a simple, cost-effective way to enhance your smile without requiring more invasive procedures.

## The Dental Bonding Process at Florida Dental Studio

When you visit Florida Dental Studio for dental bonding in Pompano Beach, you can expect a comfortable, efficient experience. Here’s what the process typically involves:

1. **Consultation:** Dr. Gavilla or Dr. Gonzalez will examine your teeth and discuss your goals to determine if bonding is the right option for you.
2. **Preparation:** We need very little preparation for dental bonding. We’ll select a resin color that matches your natural teeth and slightly roughens the tooth surface to help the bonding material adhere.
3. **Application:** The resin is applied to the tooth and carefully molded into the desired shape.
4. **Curing:** A special light hardens the resin and bonds it to your tooth.
5. **Finishing Touches:** Once the resin has hardened, your dentist will shape and polish it to perfectly match your natural teeth.

The entire process usually takes 30 to 60 minutes per tooth, and you can resume normal activities immediately after the procedure.
