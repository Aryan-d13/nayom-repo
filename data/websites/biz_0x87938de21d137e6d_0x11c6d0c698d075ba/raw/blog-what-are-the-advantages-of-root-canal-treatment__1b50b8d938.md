## What is a Root Canal?

Root canal treatment is a surgical procedure that removes the infected pulp around the end of a tooth and replaces it with a filling. It can be performed as an outpatient procedure, and patients should experience minimal discomfort.

Root canals are not pleasant to think about, but they are necessary to save your teeth from further damage. If you experience tooth pain that lasts for more than two weeks, you should contact a dentist near you for an appointment.

## What are the Benefits of Root Canal Treatment?

Root canal treatment has multiple benefits, including:

1. It stops the spread of infectionA root canal is a treatment for a tooth infection. To prevent the spread of infection, the dentist will remove the infected tooth and clean it thoroughly.After the root canal treatment, the dentist will fill it up with a temporary filling material that fits within them. The holes are sealed with a permanent filling material after a few weeks.The root canal stops the infection from spreading by removing bacteria-containing debris from inside your tooth and sealing off its entrance ways into your body.
2. It Prevents Tooth ExtractionA root canal is a procedure that removes diseased tissue in the center of your root. It helps to prevent the need for extraction.Tooth extraction is a dental problem, and it can lead to other problems like bone deterioration.A root canal procedure removes the infected pulp and seals off the teeth. This will stop the spread of infection and tooth damage. Remember, tooth extraction is not a reversible procedure, and you will rely on other restorative procedures to replace your teeth.
3. It is a pain-free procedureMany patients prefer root canals over extractions because they are a more predictable treatment. They typically only require a day or two of recovery instead of weeks. Additionally, root canals are painless and have no discomfort during recovery.Root canals are generally painless as they don’t involve tearing or cutting the gum tissue; this means that patients should not experience post-operative discomfort or problems chewing soft foods for at least four weeks after treatment.
4. It offers a visually appealing resultThe final step of the root canal treatment is fixing dental crowns. These are caps that cover the teeth to restore the strength of the teeth and also their appearance. Although the crowns are available in different materials, the porcelain crowns are often used because they resemble your teeth.

## What are the Risks of Not Getting a Root Canal Treatment?

The risk of not getting one is that it can lead to more serious dental problems, which will require much more expensive treatments down the line.

## What Happens When You Get a Root Canal?

Because it can be a painful and lengthy procedure, many people avoid getting a root canal. But, people need to know what happens during this complicated dental surgery.

The dentist will clean out the infected tooth before drilling into the cavity or pulp chamber. Once this has been done, he will drill into the center of where the infection is located using a surgical-grade instrument.

A bio-compatible material is used to fill the teeth and seal them. A temporary filling is also used as the dental crown is crafted.

You may experience discomfort, swelling, and pain after the procedure. These dental issues will clear after a while. However, if swelling persists or you experience fever, visit an [**emergency dentist in Omaha**](https://andingfamilydental.com/) for assistance.

## What is the Root Canal Aftercare Tips?

Proper aftercare is required to hasten the healing process. You will need to gently brush, eat soft food that doesn’t require chewing, avoid acidic beverages or foods like coffee or orange juice, etc. Also, schedule regular cleanings with your dentist.
