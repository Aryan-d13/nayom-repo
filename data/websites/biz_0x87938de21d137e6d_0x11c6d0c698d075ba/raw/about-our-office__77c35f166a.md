## Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry is all about improving people’s smiles. There’s no reason to live with the consequences of trauma to the face or stained teeth. Cosmetic dentistry includes multiple procedures that can be used to restore a patient’s beautiful smile.

Procedures like [teeth whitening,](https://andingfamilydental.com/service/teeth-whitening-omaha/) [veneers](https://andingfamilydental.com/service/dental-veneers-omaha/), and teeth cleaning fall under cosmetic dentistry. If you’re not happy with your smile and are looking for improvements, we’ll tell you what we think would be best after examining you.
