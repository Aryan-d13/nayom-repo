Q: How is professional whitening different from store-bought kits?
A: Professional teeth whitening uses dentist-supervised treatment and stronger whitening agents than most over-the-counter products. This often allows for faster, more consistent, and more predictable results while helping protect your oral health.

Q: How much whiter can my teeth get?
A: Results vary depending on the type and severity of staining, but many patients notice their teeth become several shades whiter after professional treatment. A dental evaluation can help determine the expected outcome for your smile.

Q: Will teeth whitening cause sensitivity?
A: Some patients experience temporary tooth sensitivity during or after whitening treatment. This is usually mild and short-lived, and professional whitening systems are designed to help minimize discomfort whenever possible.

Q: How long do whitening results last?
A: Teeth whitening results can last from several months to a few years, depending on oral hygiene habits, diet, tobacco use, and routine dental care. Periodic touch-up treatments may help maintain a brighter smile.
