Q: What is a smile makeover?
A: A smile makeover is a customized treatment plan that combines cosmetic and restorative dental procedures to improve the appearance, function, and overall health of your smile. Treatments may include veneers, crowns, teeth whitening, Invisalign, or dental implants.

Q: How long can a smile makeover take?
A: The length of a smile makeover depends on the treatments involved and your individual goals. Some procedures can be completed in a single visit, while more comprehensive treatment plans may require several appointments over a period of weeks or months.

Q: Can a smile makeover fix crooked or missing teeth?
A: Yes. A smile makeover can address crooked, misaligned, or missing teeth using treatments such as Invisalign clear aligners, dental implants, bridges, crowns, or veneers, depending on your specific needs.

Q: Will my new smile look natural?
A: Yes. Modern smile makeover treatments use high-quality materials and personalized treatment planning to create results that blend naturally with your facial features, tooth shape, and existing smile.

Q: Is a smile makeover painful?
A: Most smile makeover procedures involve minimal discomfort. Local anesthesia and modern dental techniques help keep patients comfortable throughout treatment, and recovery varies depending on the procedures performed.
