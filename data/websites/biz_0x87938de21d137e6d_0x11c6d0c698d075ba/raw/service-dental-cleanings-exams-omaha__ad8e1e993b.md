Q: What is included in a professional dental cleaning in Omaha?
A: A professional dental cleaning typically includes the removal of plaque and tartar buildup, polishing of the teeth, flossing, and an evaluation of your oral health. Regular cleanings help maintain healthy teeth and gums while reducing the risk of dental problems.

Q: How long can a dental cleaning appointment take?
A: Most routine dental cleaning appointments take between 45 minutes and one hour. The exact length may vary depending on the amount of plaque or tartar present and your individual oral health needs.

Q: Are dental cleanings painful?
A: Dental cleanings are generally comfortable and painless for most patients. Those with sensitive teeth, gum inflammation, or significant buildup may experience mild discomfort, but gentle techniques are used to help ensure a positive experience.

Q: What is the difference between a cleaning and an exam?
A: A dental cleaning focuses on removing plaque, tartar, and surface stains from the teeth. A dental exam involves evaluating your teeth, gums, and oral tissues for signs of cavities, gum disease, and other oral health concerns.
