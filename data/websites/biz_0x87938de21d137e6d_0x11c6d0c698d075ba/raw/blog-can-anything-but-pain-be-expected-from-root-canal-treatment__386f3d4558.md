## What Precisely Is Root Canal Treatment?

It can prevent further tooth damage and general health problems.

Our teeth are structured uniquely. Aside from the visible outer part, other structures inside the teeth allow us to perform various dental functions. These structures are essential and can become infected if we don’t take good care of your dental health.

The root canals in our teeth accommodate the blood vessels, nerve tissues, and most importantly, the dental pulp, which is responsible for teeth growth and development in children. However, a diseased dental pulp or tissue needs to go to prevent unwanted health complications. That is when root canal therapy is necessary.

Although there are other dental treatments for teeth infections, a root canal is one of the best ways to preserve your natural tooth and keep you safe from further infections.

## Why Do I Need A Root Canal Therapy?

Your **[dentist in Omaha](https://andingfamilydental.com/)** can ask you to get a root canal treatment or endodontic treatment for various reasons. Let’s see what these are.

If you have a broken tooth or cracked, microbes that cause infection can quickly get into the tooth’s roots. That is because the outer layer protecting the inner tooth structure is no longer viable. Once the infection gets into the dental pulp, it can cause the following dental problems:

* Severe toothache
* Tooth sensitivity
* Swollen gums
* Bone death around the top of infected tooth’ root’s
* Pus between the gums

If you want to avoid these nasty dental issues, it’s best to get root canal treatment.

## Performing Root Canal Treatment

To get a root canal treatment, you can consult our dentist in 68132. **[Dr. Michelle Chang](https://andingfamilydental.com/doctor/dr-michelle-chang/)** will take you through all the processes for your root canal treatment.

Your dentist will check your mouth before treatment begins. This helps them determine the exact location of the infection and see if there are other essential issues to note. You should tell your dentist about your medical history and any drugs you are currently taking to be safe.

If you are deemed fit for the procedure, your dentist will begin by administering a local anesthetic to desensitize the area, thereby making you numb to any discomfort or pain. Hence, the procedure is a relatively painless one.

Next, your dentist would access the infected tooth’s root and take out the decayed dental pulp. After the decayed dental pulp and tissues are gone, your dentist would clean, fill and seal the area to prevent the site from getting reinfected.

You can expect your dentist to recommend a dental crown after the process is complete. That is because the tooth can become delicate after the dental pulp is gone. The dental pulp forms a form of support for the tooth. Although the ligament that connects the tooth to the bone can support the tooth, the tooth can become weak over time. Attaching a crown would help protect the tooth against future damages.

After the procedure, your dentist would also put you through the different ways to protect your teeth and prevent infections from getting into your tooth’ roots again.

Maintenance after a root canal is as vital as the procedure itself. If infections get into the tooth’ root again, you might get another root canal treatment. If the condition is too complicated, a tooth extraction (removal of the infected tooth from its socket) might be the last resort.

Practice good oral hygiene and visit your dentist regularly for dental checkups to prevent this.


Root canal treatment or endodontic treatment is an oral treatment that involves the extraction of infections from the tooth roots.
