## FAQ's

[What dental issues can veneers correct?](# "What dental issues can veneers correct?")

Veneers are a versatile cosmetic solution used to mask chips, deep stains, or minor gaps. Many patients in the 68124 area choose them to create a more uniform, symmetrical look for teeth that are slightly crooked or misshapen.

[How long does the veneer process take?](# "How long does the veneer process take?")

The transformation typically requires two visits to our Omaha office. During the first, we prepare the teeth and take impressions, and during the second, Dr. Chang meticulously bonds the custom-made porcelain shells to your teeth for a permanent finish.

[Do porcelain veneers look like natural teeth?](# "Do porcelain veneers look like natural teeth?")

Yes. Modern porcelain mimics the light-reflecting properties of natural enamel. We customize the shade and shape of each veneer to ensure your new smile looks seamless and complements your facial features.

[Are dental veneers a permanent solution?](# "Are dental veneers a permanent solution?")

Since a small amount of enamel must be removed to ensure a flush fit, the procedure is considered irreversible. However, with proper care and routine checkups at Anding Family Dental, high-quality veneers can last a decade or longer.

[How should I care for my new veneers?](# "How should I care for my new veneers?")

Maintaining veneers is simple: brush and floss daily and visit us for regular cleanings. While porcelain is highly stain-resistant, we recommend avoiding biting down on exceptionally hard objects to prevent chipping.
