### Come Spill the Tea

### **What happens when Thirsty Thursday meets Afternoon Tea? You will get Tipsy Tea!**

#### **Thursdays | 3 PM – 6 PM**

#### Three-Tier Towers | Spritzes | Live Entertainment | River Views

**TIPSY TEA (/ˈtɪpsi tiː/) (noun)**

**A social gathering featuring savory & sweet towers, spritzes, and good conversation,
traditionally enjoyed on Thursday afternoons by the river.**

You can choose between River Tower ($55 per person) or Sunset Tower ($85 per person).
PS! * *Caviar Bites are only included with Sunset Tower.
For reservation $25 pre-payment is required.*

Elia on the River is adding a playful twist on two beloved traditions - the classic afternoon tea experience and everyone’s favorite excuse to start the weekend early.

Every Thursday from 3 PM to 6 PM, the Miami River becomes the place to be.
With three-tier towers, spritzes, live entertainment, and riverfront views designed to make Thursday afternoons
feel a little more special.

#### **But forget the teacups.** At Tipsy Tea, towers arrive stacked with sweet & savory bites, Spritzes replace the tea service, and the soundtrack will be upbeat.
