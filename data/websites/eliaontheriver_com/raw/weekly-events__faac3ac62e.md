*Experience unforgettable weekly events at Elia on the River. From live salsa nights and elegant jazz brunches to high-energy DJ sets and ladies’ nights, enjoy vibrant entertainment, handcrafted cocktails, and Southern Italian flavors at Miami’s premier waterfront destination.*


## WEEKLY EVENTS

* ## Viva Salsa Mondays

  Mondays | 7:00 PM

  **Live band playing salsa music**. Dance the night away by the river with Latin rhythms and Southern Italian flavors.
* ## Wednesdays at Elia

  Wednesdays | 7:00 PM

  Enjoy some mid-week beats with **DJ Chacalsen**. paired with Elia’s handcrafted cocktails & delicious food.
* ## Tipsy Tea Thursdays

  Thursdays | 3:00 PM

  Three-tier towers, spritzes, cocktails, live entertainment, and riverfront views - brought to you by Aperol.
* ## Bella Notte Thursdays

  Thursdays | 7:00 PM

  Ladies' Night with **DJ**. *Complimentary spritzes for the ladies in the lounge.* The perfect midweek escape!
* ## Dolce Vita Fridays

  Fridays | 5:00 PM

  Sunset live **from live band**, followed by **DJ Ornella**. Flavorful cocktails and elegant dining.
* ## Saturdays on the River

  Saturdays | 8:00 PM

  Live violinist **Kiara Fernandez** & a **DJ**. A mix of sophistication & high-energy beats by the river of Miami.
* ## Weekend by the River

  Weekends | 11:30 AM - 5:00 PM

  Brunch favorites, **live jazz band** & bottomless drinks — the perfect riverside escape! (*last-seating 4:00 PM)
* ## Sundays at Elia

  Sundays | 6:30PM

  Close the weekend on a high note with a **DJ** — the perfect blend of lively vibes & Southern Italian flavors.
