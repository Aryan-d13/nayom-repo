### What Are Dental Crowns for Cracked Teeth?

Dental crowns are caps that fit over a damaged tooth, providing protection, structure, and strength. Made from materials such as porcelain, metal, or ceramic, dental crowns are designed to restore the tooth’s original shape and size. When a tooth is cracked, broken, or severely weakened, a dental crown is placed to prevent further damage and to improve its appearance.

### Why Do You Need a Dental Crown?

When your tooth is cracked or weakened, it can cause discomfort, increase the risk of infection, and even lead to further damage if left untreated. Here are a few reasons why you might need a [dental crown](https://www.daylightdental.com/services/dental-crowns/):

* Cracked or Fractured Teeth: A cracked tooth can expose the pulp, increasing the chance of infection. A crown helps shield the tooth and prevent further damage.
* Severe Decay: When decay compromises a large portion of the tooth, a crown can restore the tooth’s structure and functionality.
* Root Canal Therapy: After a root canal, a crown helps strengthen and protect the tooth that was hollowed out during the procedure.
* Cosmetic Reasons: If you have a tooth that is severely discolored, misshapen, or worn down, a crown can improve the appearance of your smile.

### The Benefits of Dental Crowns

Dental crowns offer several benefits that can improve both your oral health and self-esteem. Here’s why they’re worth considering:

* Restored Functionality: Crowns provide strength to a damaged tooth, enabling it to function just like a natural tooth. You’ll be able to bite, chew, and speak without discomfort.
* Improved Appearance: Crowns can be customized to match the natural color and shape of your existing teeth, blending seamlessly for a beautiful smile.
* Long-lasting Solution: With proper care, dental crowns can last for years, providing a durable and long-term solution for damaged teeth.
* Prevents Further Damage: Crowns act as a protective shield, preventing cracks from worsening and avoiding the need for more extensive dental work later.

### The Process of Getting a Dental Crown

The process of getting a dental crown typically takes two visits to your dentist. Here’s what you can expect:

* Initial Consultation: During your first visit, your dentist will assess your tooth and take [X-rays](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diagnostics/11199-dental-x-rays) to check for any underlying issues. They will also shape the damaged tooth to create space for the crown.
* Impressions: After reshaping the tooth, your dentist will take impressions to create a custom crown. A temporary crown may be placed over your tooth to protect it until the permanent crown is ready.
* Final Placement: On your second visit, your dentist will place the permanent crown and ensure that it fits comfortably and matches your natural teeth.

### FAQs

### 1. How long do dental crowns last?

Dental crowns can last between 10 and 15 years with proper care. Regular brushing, flossing, and routine dental checkups will help maintain the longevity of your crown.

### 2. Are dental crowns noticeable?

No, dental crowns are custom-made to match the color and shape of your natural teeth. They blend seamlessly into your smile, making them virtually indistinguishable from your other teeth.

### 3. Can I eat normally with a dental crown?

Yes, after your crown is placed, you can resume eating and drinking as usual. However, it’s advisable to avoid hard or sticky foods, as they can potentially damage the crown.

### 4. Are dental crowns covered by insurance?

Coverage for dental crowns varies depending on your insurance plan. It’s a good idea to contact your provider to verify your policy details.

### 5. What should I do if my crown feels loose or uncomfortable?

If your crown becomes loose or uncomfortable, schedule an appointment with your dentist immediately. They can fix the issue and ensure your crown is securely in place.
