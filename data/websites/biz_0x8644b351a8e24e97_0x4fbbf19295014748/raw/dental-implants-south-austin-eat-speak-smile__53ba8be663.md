## What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants or endosseous implants are titanium fixtures inserted into your jawbone through an elective surgical procedure to function as artificial tooth roots. After the implant has successfully bonded to your jawbone, a crown, bridge, or denture is placed on top of it, providing the functionality and esthetics of your original teeth. The success of implants largely depends on their ability to fuse with your jawbone, a process called osseointegration. This helps provide stable and solid implants, enhancing your ability to chew, speak, and smile.

Dental implants offer a permanent solution that both looks and feels like natural teeth. Unlike traditional dentures, a [dental implant in South Austin](https://www.daylightdental.com/services/dental-implants/) stays securely in place, making it a dependable option for long-term restoration.

## The Dental Implant Process

Generally, undergoing an endosseous implant procedure involves a series of steps, though the outcome is well worth the process. Here’s what you should expect in your process of getting an implant:

* **Initial Consultation:** Your dentist will first examine your [oral health](https://www.healthline.com/health/dental-and-oral-health) condition, take X-rays, and explain your options. They will also ensure that your jaw has enough bone density to hold an implant.
* **Placement of the Implant:** An endosseous implant is surgically placed into your jawbone, and over time, it will integrate with the bone through a process called osseointegration.
* **Abutment Placement:** After the implant fully integrates with the bone, an abutment is placed on the implant. The abutment works as a connector for the implant, crown, bridge, and denture.
* **Final Restoration:** The custom-made crown, bridge, or denture is placed on the abutment, completing the final restoration and enabling you to have a beautiful, functional smile.

During the entire procedure, you will be assisted at every step of the way by your dentist to ensure that the implants are functioning correctly.

## Questions People Ask About Dental Implants

### 1. **How long do dental implants last?**

Dental implants can last a lifetime with proper care. The crown or restoration may need to be replaced every 10-15 years, but the implant itself is permanent.

### 2. **Are dental implants painful?**

The procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia, so discomfort is minimal. Most patients report only mild soreness after the procedure, which can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers.

### 3. **Can I get dental implants if I have gum disease?**

Gum disease must be treated before dental implants can be placed. Your dentist will assess your gum health and may recommend a cleaning or other treatments before proceeding with the implant process.

### 4. **How long does the dental implant process take?**

The entire process typically takes several months, as the implant needs time to integrate with the bone. Your dentist will give you a timeline specific to your case during your consultation.

### 5. **Can dental implants replace multiple teeth?**

Yes, dental implants can replace multiple teeth. Implants can support bridges or dentures, providing a secure and functional solution for several missing teeth.
