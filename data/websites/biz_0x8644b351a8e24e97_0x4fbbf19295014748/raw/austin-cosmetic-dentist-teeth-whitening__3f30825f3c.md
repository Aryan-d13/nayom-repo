## How Teeth Whitening Works

Teeth whitening is a simple yet effective technique, particularly when a cosmetic dentist is involved. A cosmetic dentist uses stronger whitening products to provide faster results than over-the-counter kits. Here is what teeth bleaching involves:

### Application of Whitening Gel

A whitening gel is then applied to the surface of your teeth. The gel contains potent whitening compounds that can break down stains and discolorations on your tooth enamel.

### Activation with Light or Heat

Once the gel has been applied, a specific light or heat is shone to activate the whitening agents. This facilitates the whitening process, allowing the gel to work faster and more efficiently.

### Customized Treatment

Your cosmetic dentist will assess your teeth before the treatment to establish whether whitening is suitable for you. If you have sensitive teeth, you can get your teeth whitened, and the process will be tailored to your individual needs.

## How Long Do Teeth-Whitening Results Last?

Results from professional teeth bleaching can last from three months to one year, depending on how well you care for your teeth after treatment. Although your teeth are bound to develop some discoloration after this period, you can help the treatment last longer by taking good care of your teeth.

It is also important to avoid habits such as smoking, as this can greatly affect the brightness of your teeth. Regular dental checkups with your dentist can help you maintain a bright, fresh smile.

## Questions People Ask About Teeth Whitening

### **What is the difference between over-the-counter teeth whitening and professional whitening?**

Over-the-counter products contain lower concentrations of whitening agents, which may lead to slower, less noticeable results. Professional whitening, performed by a cosmetic dentist, uses stronger agents and provides faster, longer-lasting results tailored to your teeth.

### **How long does a teeth whitening treatment take?**

In-office teeth whitening treatments at Daylight Dental South Austin usually take about 1 hour. You can leave our office with a brighter smile in just one visit!

### **Is teeth whitening safe for everyone?**

Teeth whitening is generally safe for most individuals. However, it’s important to consult our cosmetic dentist to ensure it’s suitable for your oral health, especially if you have sensitive teeth or other dental issues.

### **How long will the whitening results last?**

Professional teeth whitening can last anywhere from several months to a year, depending on your oral habits and lifestyle. Regular touch-ups and good oral hygiene can help maintain the results.

### **Can teeth whitening damage my enamel?**

No, when performed properly under a cosmetic dentist’s supervision, teeth whitening does not damage your enamel. In fact, the procedure is safe and effective when customized to your specific dental needs.

### **Will teeth whitening work on crowns or veneers?**

Teeth whitening treatments are most effective on natural teeth. Crowns, veneers, and other restorations won’t respond to whitening agents, so if you have them, you may want to discuss alternative options for achieving a uniform smile.
