## **What Does “Non-Invasive” Mean in The Dental Field?**

Non-invasive treatment doesn’t always mean no contact – it simply means there’s minimal to no damage to your natural tooth, you’re not having surgery, and you’ll have minimal recovery. It’s about optimizing function and appearance while minimizing the harm done to your oral health.

A [cosmetic dentist in Austin, TX](https://www.daylightdental.com/services/cosmetic-dentistry/), can evaluate your teeth and recommend the right option based on your specific concerns. Not every treatment fits every patient, so a proper consultation always comes first.

## **The 5 Dental Procedures Worth Knowing About**

**Teeth Whitening**

It’s the most popular and sought-after cosmetic dental treatment, and there’s a reason: it’s fast, efficient, and non-invasive! Unlike DIY kits you pick up at your local convenience store, professional in-office teeth whitening solutions offer a higher-quality solution that is more efficient at tackling stains from coffee, red wine, tobacco, and even everyday aging!

You’ll notice results immediately after your initial appointment, and you don’t have to wait long to resume normal activities! If you live around the South Congress or Mueller areas and want to improve the aesthetics of your smile before an event or just for a self-esteem boost, this would be a great place to start!

**Tooth-Colored fillings**

Old metal fillings don’t have to stay forever. Replacing old, metal amalgam fillings is easier than ever and looks natural. Tooth-colored composite fillings match your natural tooth so perfectly, and are an ideal option for both new and old cavities.

* No metal, dark patches
* Bonds directly to the tooth
* Less drilling is required compared to traditional fillings
* Safely durable enough to use during normal activities

Who knew that fillings would one day be classified as a cosmetic dentistry option- but alas, here we are!

**Dental Bonding**

Arguably one of the most underrated of all dental cosmetic procedures that are available today! Bonding consists of a tooth-colored resin that can be applied to the tooth to correct minor imperfections like chips, cracks, and even large gaps in between your teeth.

This procedure usually lasts 30-60 minutes per tooth, doesn’t normally require drilling or anesthesia, and requires no recovery time. Compared to other, more costly treatments such as porcelain veneers, bonding is a very reasonable option that will last for years if properly cared for.

**Invisalign Clear Braces**

Worried about having to get braces? Worry no more! Traditional braces were once considered the most effective method for straightening misaligned teeth, but as many know, [Invisalign](https://www.daylightdental.com/services/invisalign/) clear braces are the perfect alternative. This method uses removable, clear aligners that gradually move your teeth into the perfect position over the course of treatment.

This procedure is ideal for all professionals who need an effective cosmetic treatment that isn’t overly burdensome.

* Virtually invisible when worn
* Easy to remove during eating and brushing your teeth
* Fewer dentist visits are required than with traditional metal braces
* Efficient enough for moderate to minor alignment issues

Treatment time will vary depending on your concerns, but many patients see results as early as 6-18 months! If you’re an Austin professional looking to optimize your oral aesthetics, Invisalign clear braces are the answer.

**Porcelain Dental Crowns**

Don’t be afraid of porcelain crowns. They aren’t the drastic procedure they were once presumed to be. Porcelain crowns are designed and shaped to look and function like your natural teeth and are an effective option for cracked teeth or those that are no longer strong enough for fillings to be effective.

Many dental offices in Austin today offer “same-day” porcelain crowns using CAD/CAM technology. This process helps eliminate the hassle of additional dental visits and temporary crowns, and ensures a better-fitting result. Porcelain crowns will also last for 10-15 years with good dental hygiene.

## **How to Know Which Treatment Is Right for You**

There is no one-size-fits-all solution. The best treatment for you will depend on:

* The state of your teeth and gums at the time of treatment
* Your desired outcomes, whether it be functional, aesthetic, or both
* Budget and time constraints
* Whether or not you have existing dental work that must be taken into consideration

A qualified cosmetic dentist in Austin, TX, can discuss your options after a thorough examination. Going into treatment without a full consultation can result in an undesirable outcome that doesn’t last.

## **A Few Things to Keep in Mind**

* Cosmetic dentistry is usually elective, meaning that it may not be covered by insurance. It is always a good idea to ask for a breakdown of costs.
* [Good oral hygiene](https://www.colgate.com/en-us/oral-health/adult-oral-care/what-is-good-oral-hygiene) is what will keep your treatment results lasting.
* Some treatments, such as bonding or whitening, may require follow-up procedures.
* Not everyone is a candidate for all treatments. Issues such as gum disease must be addressed before certain treatments are performed.

## **Common Questions Most People Ask**

### **How long does teeth whitening last?**

Professional whitening typically lasts 1 to 3 years, depending on diet and oral hygiene habits. Touch-ups can extend results.

### **Is dental bonding painful?**

In most cases, no anesthesia is needed. The procedure is quick and generally painless unless the bonding is being applied near a decayed area.

### **Can Invisalign fix severe misalignment?**

Invisalign works well for mild to moderate cases. Severe misalignment may require traditional orthodontic treatment. A consultation will clarify which option fits your situation.

### **Are porcelain crowns covered by insurance?**

It depends on the reason for the crown. If it’s for structural restoration after decay or fracture, partial coverage is common. Purely cosmetic crowns are usually not covered.
