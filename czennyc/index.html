Section 03 · The Gathering

Good food tastes better around people.

We built Mara to be the long dinner table you wish you had at home. Unhurried meals, elbows on wood, plates passed across the center, and no one checking the time.

“Stay for another bottle.”

Tuesday through Sunday evenings in Greenpoint.

Dinner Service

Tue – Sun · 5 PM – 11 PM

Kitchen fires up at 5:00 PM with last orders taken at 10:15 PM. Bar pours late until the last story ends. Closed Mondays.

Walk-Ins Welcome

Half Room Held Every Night

We keep the entire 12-seat zinc bar and front bistro tables open for spontaneous arrivals. If there is a wait, enjoy an aperitivo outside.

Large Feasts

Tables for 6 to 14 Guests

For gatherings of 6 or more, our kitchen serves a multi-course family-style feast featuring whole roasted fish, pasta, and shared carafes.
